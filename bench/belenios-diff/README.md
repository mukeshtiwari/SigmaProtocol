# Differential testing against belenios-tool

Compares the official Belenios command-line verifier, `belenios-tool
election verify`, with the certified verifier of this repository
([Beleniosverifier](/src/Executable/Beleniosverifier/main.ml), over
[BeleniosTally.v](/src/Backend/BeleniosTally.v)) on 961 Belenios elections:
honest ones, and tampered ones that are hash-consistent archives (so that
they get past the archive integrity check), with re-signed ballots and,
where a forgery needs them, valid proofs built with the voters' credentials
and the trustees' private keys. The findings are listed under Results below.

## Reproducing

```sh
bench/belenios-diff/run.sh            # SEED=1 JOBS=4 by default; work directory bench/belenios-diff/_work
```

`run.sh` creates the opam switch `belenios-diff` from the frozen export
[belenios-diff.opam-switch](belenios-diff.opam-switch) (OCaml 5.4.1; the
default switch is not touched), clones Belenios and checks out commit
`50a79aed0d34d77a406958dd5a8cb77fe2f32b30` (tag 3.2.0, the version whose
data format the certified verifier reads), builds `belenios-tool`, builds
the certified verifier (default switch), generates the cases from the
committed fixtures with `cases.py` and the seed, and runs both verifiers
(`run_cases.py`). Results are written to [results/](results):
`summary.tsv` and one TSV per stage (case, expected verdict, both verdicts,
the description and each verifier's last message). Requirements: opam,
git, python3 (no packages), jq, libsodium and pkg-config (macOS: `brew
install libsodium pkgconf`).

The fixtures are five elections generated by `belenios-tool` 3.2.0 with
[gen_election.sh](gen_election.sh), which follows Belenios's
`tests/tool/demo.sh`, from the inputs in [elections/](elections). They are
committed because belenios-tool draws its randomness from `/dev/urandom`:
`run.sh --regen` regenerates them (different archives, the same elections).
Everything else is deterministic: two runs with the same seed produce
byte-identical case archives (`_work/cases.sha256`) and identical result
tables, which was checked (two complete runs, each building Belenios from a
fresh clone). The switch was created once from scratch with `opam switch
create` and the dependencies of Belenios's opam files plus base64,
cohttp-lwt-unix, digestif, hex, lwt and uri (which its dune files need);
the export freezes that state.

| fixture | voters | trustees | questions | ballots |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| demo | 5, weighted (1e9 … 9e10) | 3 | 2 answers min 0 max 1; 2 answers + blank, min 1 max 1 | 5 + 3 revotes |
| multi | 12 | 2 | 4 answers min 1 max 3; 3 answers + blank, min 2 max 2; 2 answers min 0 max 2 | 12 + 2 revotes (one blank) |
| single | 1 | 1 | 2 answers min 1 max 1; 3 answers + blank, min 1 max 2 | 1 |
| empty | 2 | 1 | as single | 0 |
| complex | 10 | 1 | 8 answers min 1 max 1; 131 answers min 1 max 10; 19 answers min 1 max 10 | 10 |

Only homomorphic questions and "Single" trustees: the certified verifier
does not support threshold trustees or non-homomorphic (mix-net) questions.

## The cases

[belenios_lib.py](belenios_lib.py) implements, from the Belenios
specification of the pinned release, the archive (Belenios's reproducible
tar headers, the hash-named documents and the event chain, rewritten after
every change), Ed25519, credentials, ballot signatures, the individual,
overall and blank-vote proofs, the encrypted tally, partial decryptions and
the result, with seeded randomness. There is no certified Belenios prover
in this repository; this Python prover is an independent implementation,
and the honest elections it builds are accepted by both verifiers.

- **honest** (9): the five fixtures; demo, multi and single re-cast with the
  Python prover's ballots (tally, partial decryptions and result recomputed
  with the trustees' keys); boundary and blank votes.
- **field** (749): one random occurrence of every field of every document
  (election, trustees, credentials, a ballot, the tally header and tally, a
  partial decryption and its header, the result), mutated by type: points
  (another point, negated, generator, identity, order 2, non-canonical, off
  the curve, upper case, truncated), scalars (+1, 0, l, +l, −1, random, JSON
  number, leading zero), integers, strings, booleans, document references;
  ballot mutants are re-signed with the voter's credential.
- **struct** (99): events dropped, duplicated, reordered, a second
  `EndBallots`; ballot JSON shapes and encodings (extra or missing fields,
  vectors one short or long, blank proofs where they do not belong,
  reordered or pretty-printed JSON, numbers as JSON numbers, Unicode escapes,
  Base64 padding); credentials, trustees and result shapes.
- **forge** (104): single-check forgeries, each with its control (the same
  construction without the fault, which both must accept): the signature
  hash, the ballot's election hash and UUID, proofs for another election,
  an unlisted credential, the tally of `last(B)` (a ballot left out, the
  first ballot of a revoting credential, every ballot of a revoting
  credential), weights, `num_tallied`, `total_weight`, the result, swapped
  partial-decryption owners; Fiat–Shamir binding (an individual proof whose
  challenge is computed without S0, without the ciphertext, with another
  credential, with the announcements reversed); ballot stuffing and vote
  override with invalid ballots.

## Results (SEED=1)

| stage | cases | both accept | both reject | belenios-tool only accepts | certified only accepts | disagreements | agreeing, not as expected |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| honest | 9 | 9 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| field | 749 | 0 | 749 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| struct | 99 | 9 | 77 | 5 | 8 | 13 | 7 |
| forge | 104 | 50 | 18 | 36 | 0 | 36 | 6 |

Every disagreement has a root cause:

- belenios-tool accepts, certified rejects (41): **`election verify` does
  not verify ballots** (36: forged signatures, hashes, UUIDs, Fiat–Shamir
  inputs, ballot stuffing, vote override: `verify` drops the promise of
  the ballot check, `ignore (Lazy.force verified_ballots)` in
  `src/tool/tool_election.ml`, also in 3.3.0 and on master) and **ballots before
  a second `EndBallots` are not tallied** (2: belenios-tool tallies only the run of
  Ballot events ending at the last one, and accepts a second `EndBallots`); an election without
  a `Result` (3: belenios-tool verifies an election in progress, the
  certified verifier requires the result, by design).
- certified accepts, belenios-tool rejects (8): the certified driver does not
  check the event sequence (duplicated partial decryption or ballot events,
  Ballot events after `EndBallots`).
- agreeing but not as expected (13): neither checks `num_tallied` and
  `total_weight` (6) or the order of `EndBallots` and
  `EncryptedTally` (6) and a single ballot moved after `EndBallots` (1).

Run time: about 100 s per complete run (build included, 4 jobs, Apple M3 Pro).
