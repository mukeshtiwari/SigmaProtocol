Modular exponentiation on binary naturals

This file defines and verifies modular exponentiation e ^ n mod w on the binary natural numbers N of the Rocq standard library. It is the executable exponentiation used by the multiplicative group Z_p^* development (Zpstar.v), by Fermat-based inverses (Fermat.v) and by the Schnorr-group order checks (SchnorrOrder.v); after extraction it becomes plain big-integer arithmetic.
Three implementations are given and proved to agree:
  • Npow_mod (via repeat_op_ntimes_rec): left-to-right square-and-multiply that recurses over the bits of the positive exponent. It performs one squaring per bit and one extra multiplication per set bit, so it uses Θ(log n) modular multiplications; the inductive relation complexity_repeat_op_ntimes_rec records this cost and correct_complexity_repeat_op_ntimes_rec proves that the recursion depth is exactly N.log2 n.
  • Npow_mod_constant_space (via repeat_op_ntimes_acc): a tail-recursive, accumulator-based right-to-left variant that runs in constant stack space.
  • Npow_mod_unary: the obvious specification, multiplying n times; it is used only as a reference model in proofs.
The bridge lemmas are npow_mod_npow_constant_eqv (fast = constant space), npow_mod_exp_unary_binary_eqv (unary = fast) and, most importantly for client files, zmod_nmod, which identifies Npow_mod with the standard library's Zpow_mod on integers so that number-theoretic facts proved over Z (for example Fermat's little theorem) can be transported to the executable function.
Many lemmas carry a hypothesis prime (Z.of_N w) although they only need w ≠ 0 (or w ≥ 2); primality is simply the setting in which they are used.

repeat_op_ntimes_rec e n w computes e ^ n mod w for a positive exponent n by recursion on its binary representation: for n = 2p it squares the result for p, for n = 2p+1 it squares and multiplies by e, and for n = 1 it returns e mod w. Every intermediate value is reduced modulo w, so operands stay below w.
  Fixpoint repeat_op_ntimes_rec (e : N) (n : positive) (w : N) : N :=
    match n with
    | xH ⇒ N.modulo e w
    | xO p ⇒ let ret := repeat_op_ntimes_rec e p w in
        N.modulo (ret × ret) w
    | xI p ⇒ let ret := repeat_op_ntimes_rec e p w in
        N.modulo (e × (ret × ret)) w
    end.

Npow_mod e n w is e ^ n mod w for an arbitrary n : N. The case n = 0 returns 1 without reducing it modulo w (so for w = 1 the result is 1, not 0); this is harmless because w is a prime in every use.
  Definition Npow_mod (e : N) (n w : N) :=
    match n with
    | N0 ⇒ Npos xH
    | Npos p ⇒ repeat_op_ntimes_rec e p w
    end.

complexity_repeat_op_ntimes_rec e t n r k is a big-step cost semantics for repeat_op_ntimes_rec: it says that the recursion on exponent n modulo t returns r after k recursive (doubling) steps. Each constructor mirrors one branch of the function and increments the step count by one.
  Inductive complexity_repeat_op_ntimes_rec
    (e t : N) : positive → N → N → Prop :=
  | xH_case : complexity_repeat_op_ntimes_rec e t xH
      (N.modulo e t) N0
  | xO_case p ret w :
      complexity_repeat_op_ntimes_rec e t p ret w →
      complexity_repeat_op_ntimes_rec e t (xO p)
      (N.modulo (ret × ret) t) (N.succ w)
  | xI_case p ret w :
      complexity_repeat_op_ntimes_rec e t p ret w →
      complexity_repeat_op_ntimes_rec e t (xI p)
      (N.modulo (e × (ret × ret)) t) (N.succ w).

Complexity bound: repeat_op_ntimes_rec e n w is related by complexity_repeat_op_ntimes_rec to its own result with step count N.log2 n, so square-and-multiply uses ⌊log₂ n⌋ doubling steps (each with at most two modular multiplications). The proof is by induction on n, using N.log2_succ_double and N.log2_double.
  Lemma correct_complexity_repeat_op_ntimes_rec :
    ∀ n e w,
    complexity_repeat_op_ntimes_rec e w n
      (repeat_op_ntimes_rec e n w) (N.log2 (N.pos n)).
  Proof.
    induction n;
    intros e w.
    + specialize (IHn e w).
      assert (Hwt : ((N.pos n~1) = (2 × (N.pos n) + 1))%N).
      cbn; nia.
      rewrite Hwt; clear Hwt.
      rewrite N.log2_succ_double.
      remember ((N.log2 (N.pos n))) as p.
      cbn.
      apply xI_case.
      exact IHn.
      nia.
    + specialize (IHn e w).
      assert (Hwt : ((N.pos n~0) = (2 × (N.pos n)))%N).
      cbn; nia.
      rewrite Hwt; clear Hwt.
      rewrite N.log2_double.
      remember ((N.log2 (N.pos n))) as p.
      cbn.
      apply xO_case.
      exact IHn.
      nia.
    + cbn; apply xH_case.
  Qed.

Npow_mod_unary e n w is the naive specification of modular exponentiation with a unary exponent n : nat: it multiplies by e and reduces modulo w n times, starting from 1. It is linear in n and is used only as a reference for correctness proofs.
  Fixpoint Npow_mod_unary (e : N) (n : nat) (w : N) : N :=
    match n with
    | 0%nat ⇒ Npos xH
    | S n' ⇒ N.modulo (e × Npow_mod_unary e n' w) w
    end.

repeat_op_ntimes_acc e n w acc is the tail-recursive (right-to-left) variant of square-and-multiply. It scans the bits of n from the least significant one, squaring the base e at each step and multiplying it into the accumulator acc whenever the current bit is set. It computes acc · e ^ n mod w (positive_mul_group_acc_rec_connection).
  Fixpoint repeat_op_ntimes_acc
    (e : N) (n : positive) (w acc : N) : N :=
    match n with
    | xH ⇒ N.modulo (e × acc) w
    | xO p ⇒
      let ee := (N.modulo (e × e) w) in
      repeat_op_ntimes_acc ee p w acc
    | xI p ⇒
      let ee := (N.modulo (e × e) w) in
      let ea := (N.modulo (e × acc) w) in
      repeat_op_ntimes_acc ee p w ea
    end.

Squaring the base squares the result: repeat_op_ntimes_rec ((e·e) mod w) n w = (r · r) mod w where r = repeat_op_ntimes_rec e n w. This is the key step relating the right-to-left and left-to-right algorithms.
  Lemma op_pushes_out :
    ∀ n e w, prime (Z.of_N w) →
    repeat_op_ntimes_rec ((e × e) mod w) n w =
    N.modulo (
      (repeat_op_ntimes_rec e n w × repeat_op_ntimes_rec e n w)) w.
  Proof.
    induction n.
    - simpl; intros ? ? Hw.
      rewrite IHn.
      pose proof (prime_ge_2 (Z.of_N w) Hw) as Htt.
      remember (repeat_op_ntimes_rec e n w × repeat_op_ntimes_rec e n w)%N as enw.
      rewrite <- N.Div0.mul_mod_idemp_r.
      repeat rewrite N.Div0.mul_mod_idemp_l.
      repeat rewrite N.Div0.mul_mod_idemp_r.
      assert (Ht : (e × enw × (e × enw) =
        e × e × (enw × enw))%N). lia.
      rewrite Ht; clear Ht.
      repeat rewrite N.mul_assoc.
      rewrite N.Div0.mul_mod_idemp_r.
      reflexivity.
      all:(try lia; try assumption).
    - simpl; intros ? ? Hw.
      rewrite IHn.
      pose proof (prime_ge_2 (Z.of_N w) Hw) as Htt.
      reflexivity. exact Hw.
    - simpl; intros ? ? Hw.
      pose proof (prime_ge_2 (Z.of_N w) Hw) as Htt.
      rewrite N.Div0.mod_mod.
      rewrite N.Div0.mul_mod_idemp_l.
      rewrite N.Div0.mul_mod_idemp_r.
      reflexivity.
  Qed.

The accumulator version computes acc times the recursive one: repeat_op_ntimes_acc e n w acc = (acc · repeat_op_ntimes_rec e n w) mod w. Proof by induction on n, generalising e and acc, with op_pushes_out for the squaring steps.
  Lemma positive_mul_group_acc_rec_connection :
    ∀ n e w acc,
    prime (Z.of_N w) →
    repeat_op_ntimes_acc e n w acc = N.modulo (acc × repeat_op_ntimes_rec e n w) w.
  Proof.
    induction n.
    - simpl; intros ? ? ? Hw.
      pose proof (prime_ge_2 (Z.of_N w) Hw) as Htt.
      specialize (IHn (N.modulo (e × e) w) w (N.modulo (e × acc) w)).
      rewrite IHn.
      rewrite op_pushes_out.
      remember (repeat_op_ntimes_rec e n w × repeat_op_ntimes_rec e n w)%N as enw.
      rewrite N.Div0.mul_mod_idemp_l.
      repeat rewrite N.Div0.mul_mod_idemp_r.
      assert (Ht : ((acc × (e × enw) = e × acc × enw)%N)).
      lia.
      rewrite Ht; clear Ht.
      reflexivity.
      all:(try lia; try assumption).
    - simpl; intros ? ? ? Hw.
      pose proof (prime_ge_2 (Z.of_N w) Hw) as Htt.
      rewrite IHn. rewrite op_pushes_out.
      reflexivity.
      all:assumption.
    - simpl; intros ? ? ? Hw.
      pose proof (prime_ge_2 (Z.of_N w) Hw) as Htt.
      rewrite N.Div0.mul_mod_idemp_r.
      assert (Ht : (acc × e = e × acc)%N). lia.
      rewrite Ht; clear Ht.
      reflexivity.
  Qed.

Npow_mod_constant_space e n w is e ^ n mod w computed by the constant-space repeat_op_ntimes_acc with initial accumulator 1.
  Definition Npow_mod_constant_space (e : N) (n w : N) :=
    match n with
    | N0 ⇒ Npos xH
    | Npos p ⇒ repeat_op_ntimes_acc e p w 1
    end.

The two binary algorithms agree: for a prime modulus w, Npow_mod e n w = Npow_mod_constant_space e n w.
  Lemma npow_mod_npow_constant_eqv :
    ∀ n e w,
    prime (Z.of_N w) →
    Npow_mod e n w = Npow_mod_constant_space e n w.
  Proof.
    destruct n; simpl; intros ? ? Hw.
    - reflexivity.
    - pose proof positive_mul_group_acc_rec_connection p e w 1 Hw.
      pose proof (prime_ge_2 (Z.of_N w) Hw) as Htt.
      rewrite H. rewrite N.mul_1_l.
      destruct p.
      all:simpl; rewrite N.Div0.mod_mod; lia.
  Qed.

The unary power is a reduced residue: Npow_mod_unary e n w < w when w is prime (only w ≥ 2 is needed, so that 1 < w).
  Lemma Npow_mod_unary_bound :
    ∀ (n : nat) (e w : N),
    prime (Z.of_N w) →
    (Npow_mod_unary e n w < w)%N.
  Proof.
    induction n.
    - simpl; intros ? ? Hw.
      pose proof (prime_ge_2 (Z.of_N w) Hw) as Htt.
      lia.
    - simpl; intros ? ? Hw.
      apply N.mod_lt.
      pose proof (prime_ge_2 (Z.of_N w) Hw) as Htt.
      lia.
  Qed.

If N.of_nat n = 0 then n = 0.
  Lemma binnat_zero :
    ∀ (n : nat), 0%N = N.of_nat n → n = 0%nat.
  Proof.
    induction n; try lia.
  Qed.

Exponent addition law for the reference model: e ^ (n+m) mod w = ((e ^ n mod w) · (e ^ m mod w)) mod w, stated for Npow_mod_unary. It is the main tool in npow_mod_exp_unary_binary_eqv.
  Theorem Npow_mod_add_mul :
    ∀ n m e w,
    prime (Z.of_N w) →
    Npow_mod_unary e (n + m) w = N.modulo (Npow_mod_unary e n w ×
    Npow_mod_unary e m w) w.
  Proof.
    induction n.
    - intros ? ? ? Hw.
      rewrite Nat.add_0_l.
      assert (Ht : Npow_mod_unary e 0 w = Npos xH).
      simpl. reflexivity.
      rewrite Ht.
      rewrite N.mul_1_l.
      induction m.
      + simpl. rewrite N.mod_1_l.
        reflexivity.
        pose proof (prime_ge_2 (Z.of_N w) Hw) as Htt.
        lia.
      + simpl. rewrite N.Div0.mod_mod.
        reflexivity.
    - simpl; intros ? ? ? Hw.
      pose proof (prime_ge_2 (Z.of_N w) Hw) as Htt.
      rewrite IHn.
      rewrite N.Div0.mul_mod_idemp_r.
      rewrite N.Div0.mul_mod_idemp_l.
      rewrite N.mul_assoc. reflexivity.
      assumption.
  Qed.

If the binary number 2p+1 (written xI p) equals N.of_nat n, then n = 2k+1 for some k with N.pos p = N.of_nat k.
  Lemma binnat_odd :
    ∀ (p : positive) (n : nat),
    N.pos (xI p) = N.of_nat n →
    ∃ k, n = (2 × k + 1)%nat ∧ (N.pos p) = (N.of_nat k).
  Proof.
    intros p n Hp.
    destruct (Nat.Even_or_Odd n) as [H | H].
    destruct H as [k Hk].
    rewrite Hk in Hp; lia.
    destruct H as [k Hk].
    rewrite Hk in Hp. ∃ k.
    split. exact Hk. lia.
  Qed.

If the binary number 2p (written xO p) equals N.of_nat n, then n = 2k for some k with N.pos p = N.of_nat k.
  Lemma binnat_even : ∀ (p : positive) (n : nat),
    N.pos (xO p) = N.of_nat n :> N →
    ∃ k, n = (Nat.mul 2 k) ∧ (N.pos p) = (N.of_nat k).
  Proof.
    intros p n Hp.
    destruct (Nat.Even_or_Odd n) as [H | H].
    destruct H as [k Hk].
    rewrite Hk in Hp. ∃ k.
    split. exact Hk. lia.
    destruct H as [k Hk].
    rewrite Hk in Hp. lia.
  Qed.

The fast algorithm meets its specification: for a prime modulus, Npow_mod_unary e (N.to_nat n) w = Npow_mod e n w. Proof by induction on the positive exponent: the odd case splits 2p+1 as p + p + 1 and the even case 2p as p + p, each closed with Npow_mod_add_mul.
  Lemma npow_mod_exp_unary_binary_eqv :
    ∀ (n : N) e w, prime (Z.of_N w) →
    Npow_mod_unary e (N.to_nat n) w = Npow_mod e n w.
  Proof.
    destruct n.
    - simpl; intros ? ? Hw.
      reflexivity.
    - simpl; revert p.
      induction p.
      + simpl; intros ? ? Hw.
        pose proof (prime_ge_2 (Z.of_N w) Hw) as Htt.
        rewrite <-IHp.
        rewrite ZL6.
        rewrite Npow_mod_add_mul.
        rewrite N.Div0.mul_mod_idemp_r.
        reflexivity.
        exact Hw.
        exact Hw.
      + simpl; intros ? ? Hw.
        rewrite <-IHp.
        rewrite Pos2Nat.inj_xO.
        assert (Ht : (2 × Pos.to_nat p =
          Pos.to_nat p + Pos.to_nat p)%nat).
        lia. rewrite Ht.
        rewrite Npow_mod_add_mul.
        reflexivity.
        exact Hw.
        exact Hw.
      + simpl; intros ? ? Hw.
        rewrite N.mul_1_r.
        reflexivity.
  Qed.

The base may be reduced first: repeat_op_ntimes_rec e n w = repeat_op_ntimes_rec (e mod w) n w.
  Lemma mod_reduce_pow :
    ∀ n e w, prime (Z.of_N w) →
    repeat_op_ntimes_rec e n w =
    repeat_op_ntimes_rec (N.modulo e w) n w.
  Proof.
    induction n.
    - simpl; intros ? ? Hp.
      rewrite IHn.
      remember (repeat_op_ntimes_rec (e mod w) n w ×
      repeat_op_ntimes_rec (e mod w) n w)%N as t.
      rewrite N.Div0.mul_mod_idemp_l.
      reflexivity. exact Hp.
    - simpl; intros ? ? Hp.
      rewrite IHn.
      reflexivity.
      exact Hp.
    - simpl; intros ? ? Hp.
      rewrite N.Div0.mod_mod.
      reflexivity.
  Qed.

The same statement for Npow_mod: e ^ n mod w = (e mod w) ^ n mod w.
  Lemma Nmod_reduce_pow : ∀ n e w, prime (Z.of_N w) →
    Npow_mod e n w = Npow_mod (N.modulo e w) n w.
  Proof.
    destruct n.
    - simpl; intros ? ? Hp.
      reflexivity.
    - simpl; intros ? ? Hp.
      apply mod_reduce_pow.
      exact Hp.
  Qed.

If w ≡ 0 (mod p) then Npow_mod (w mod p) k p = 0 for any exponent k ≥ 2 (in fact any k ≥ 1 would do).
  Lemma wp_mod_zero :
    ∀ (w k p : N),
    prime (Z.of_N p) →
    (2 ≤ k)%N → (2 ≤ p)%N →
    (w mod p = 0)%N → (Npow_mod (w mod p) k p = 0)%N.
  Proof.
    intros ? ? ? Hp Hk Hpt Hwp.
    rewrite Hwp.
    unfold Npow_mod.
    destruct k. lia.
    clear Hk.
    induction p0.
    simpl. rewrite N.Div0.mod_0_l.
    reflexivity.
    simpl. rewrite IHp0.
    rewrite N.Div0.mod_0_l.
    reflexivity.
    simpl. rewrite N.Div0.mod_0_l.
    reflexivity.
  Qed.

If w ≡ 1 (mod p) then Npow_mod (w mod p) k p = 1 for k ≥ 2 and p ≥ 2.
  Lemma wp_mod_one :
    ∀ (w k p : N),
    prime (Z.of_N p) →
    (2 ≤ k)%N → (2 ≤ p)%N →
    (w mod p = 1)%N → (Npow_mod (w mod p) k p = 1)%N.
  Proof.
    intros ? ? ? Hp Hk Hpt Hwp.
    rewrite Hwp.
    unfold Npow_mod.
    destruct k. lia.
    clear Hk.
    induction p0.
    simpl. rewrite IHp0.
    simpl. rewrite N.mod_1_l.
    reflexivity. lia.
    simpl. rewrite IHp0.
    rewrite N.mod_1_l.
    reflexivity. lia.
    simpl. rewrite N.mod_1_l.
    reflexivity. lia.
  Qed.

Bridge to the integers: for a prime modulus w, Z.of_N (Npow_mod a b w) = Zpow_mod (Z.of_N a) (Z.of_N b) (Z.of_N w). This is the lemma client files (Zpstar.v, SchnorrOrder.v) use to move between the executable N exponentiation and Z-level number theory. The proof rewrites Zpow_mod with Zpow_mod_correct and proceeds by induction on the positive exponent, splitting Z.pow_pos with Zpower_pos_is_exp and pushing mod through products.
  Lemma zmod_nmod :
    ∀ (b a w : N),
    prime (Z.of_N w) →
    Z.of_N (Npow_mod a b w) =
    Zpow_mod (Z.of_N a) (Z.of_N b) (Z.of_N w).
  Proof.
    intros ? ? ? Hw.
    rewrite Zpow_mod_correct.
    destruct b; simpl.
    - symmetry.
      rewrite Z.mod_1_l.
      reflexivity.
      pose proof (prime_ge_2 (Z.of_N w) Hw) as Ht.
      lia.
    - revert p.
      induction p.
      + simpl.
        assert (Ht : (p~1 = p + p + 1)%positive).
        lia. rewrite Ht.
        rewrite Zpower_pos_is_exp.
        rewrite Zpower_pos_is_exp.
        rewrite Zpower_pos_1_r.
        rewrite N2Z.inj_mod.
        rewrite N2Z.inj_mul.
        rewrite N2Z.inj_mul.
        rewrite IHp.
        remember (Z.pow_pos (Z.of_N a) p) as zps.
        remember (Z.of_N a) as za.
        remember (Z.of_N w) as zw.
        assert (Hzp: zps × zps × za = za × (zps × zps)).
        lia. rewrite Hzp; clear Hzp; clear Ht.
        rewrite <-Zmult_mod_idemp_l.
        assert (Ht : (za × (zps × zps)) mod zw = (za mod zw × (zps × zps)) mod zw).
        rewrite <-Zmult_mod_idemp_l.
        reflexivity. rewrite Ht; clear Ht.
        assert (Ht : (za mod zw × (zps × zps)) mod zw =
        (za mod zw × ((zps × zps) mod zw)) mod zw).
        rewrite <-Zmult_mod_idemp_r. reflexivity.
        rewrite Ht; clear Ht.
        assert (Ht : (zps × zps) mod zw =
          (zps mod zw × (zps mod zw)) mod zw).
        rewrite <-Zmult_mod_idemp_l.
        rewrite <-Zmult_mod_idemp_r.
        reflexivity.
        rewrite Ht.
        rewrite Zmult_mod_idemp_r.
        reflexivity.
      + simpl.
        assert (Ht : (p~0 = p + p)%positive).
        lia. rewrite Ht.
        rewrite Zpower_pos_is_exp.
        rewrite N2Z.inj_mod.
        rewrite N2Z.inj_mul.
        rewrite IHp.
        remember (Z.pow_pos (Z.of_N a) p) as zps.
        remember (Z.of_N a) as za.
        remember (Z.of_N w) as zw.
        rewrite Zmult_mod_idemp_l.
        rewrite Zmult_mod_idemp_r.
        reflexivity.
      + simpl. rewrite Zpower_pos_1_r.
        rewrite N2Z.inj_mod.
        reflexivity.
    - pose proof (prime_ge_2 (Z.of_N w) Hw) as Ht.
      lia.
  Qed.

Bridge to nat: Npow_mod_unary (N.of_nat a) n (N.of_nat p) equals N.of_nat ((a ^ n) mod p) when p is prime.
  Lemma npow_mod_nat : ∀ (n a p : nat),
    prime (Z.of_nat p) →
    Npow_mod_unary (N.of_nat a) n (N.of_nat p) =
    N.of_nat (Nat.modulo (Nat.pow a n) p).
  Proof.
    induction n.
    + intros × Hp.
      pose proof prime_ge_2 (Z.of_nat p) Hp as Hf.
      simpl. rewrite Nat.mod_1_l.
      lia. lia.
    + intros × Hp.
      simpl.
      pose proof (IHn a p Hp).
      rewrite H.
      rewrite <-Nat2N.inj_mul,
      <-Nat2N.inj_mod.
      f_equal.
      rewrite Nat.Div0.mul_mod_idemp_r.
      reflexivity.
  Qed.

(a ^ n) mod p on N equals the same expression computed on nat and converted back with N.of_nat. (The primality hypothesis is unused.)
  Lemma N_to_nat_exp : ∀ (n a p : N),
    prime (Z.of_N p) →
    N.modulo (N.pow a n) p =
    N.of_nat (Nat.modulo (Nat.pow (N.to_nat a)
      (N.to_nat n)) (N.to_nat p)).
  Proof.
    intros × Hp.
    rewrite <-N2Nat.inj_pow.
    rewrite <-N2Nat.inj_mod.
    lia.
  Qed.
