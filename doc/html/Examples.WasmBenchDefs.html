WebAssembly benchmarks at the Helios parameters

These are fixed inputs for compiling the Helios voting client (Examples/HeliosFrontendIns.v) to WebAssembly with CertiRocq. The terms are a 7-candidate approval ballot at the 2048-bit Helios group of Examples/HeliosTallyIns.v under the IACR 2024 key h2024, with pinned randomness and challenges so that each compiled term is closed. They are compiled by bench/wasm/WasmBench.v (CertiRocq Compile Wasm). The same ballot term is also used by the CakeML pipeline in bench/cakeml/Driver.v.
According to bench/README.md, all terms compile, but at 2048 bits the vote and ballot modules exhaust the backend's 2 GB linear memory, because CertiRocq computes on constructor-represented binary integers without garbage collection. Only the small exponentiation probe runs. Examples/WasmBenchSmallDefs.v repeats the experiment at smaller parameters. Nothing is proved here.
