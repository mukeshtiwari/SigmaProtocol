Library Frontend.Approval

Approval voting ballots: per-candidate 0/1 proofs and an overall proof

This file formalises the voter side (the front end) of encrypted approval voting in the style of Helios. A ballot for k candidates is a vector of k votes m_i, each of which must be 0 (not approved) or 1 (approved). Every vote is encrypted separately under exponential ElGamal with public key h = g ^ x:
      enc g h m r = (g ^ r, g ^ m · h ^ r)
and is accompanied by a non-interactive-ready Sigma proof that the ciphertext encrypts either g ^ 0 or g ^ 1. The tally (see Backend/Tally.v and Examples/ApprovalIns.v) multiplies the ciphertexts candidate-wise across ballots and decrypts the sums.
The per-vote proof._ The relation proved for a ciphertext (c₁, c₂) is the disjunction
      ∃ r, (c₁, c₂) = (g ^ r, g ^ 0 · h ^ r) ∨ (c₁, c₂) = (g ^ r, g ^ 1 · h ^ r)
It is an instance of the generalised disjunctive (OR) encryption proof of Crypto/EncProof.v with two branches, which is itself the Cramer–Damgård–Schoenmakers (CRYPTO 1994) OR-composition of Chaum–Pedersen equality-of-discrete-log proofs: for the true branch i the prover runs a real Chaum–Pedersen proof that log_g c₁ = log_h (c₂ · (g ^ m_i)⁻¹), for the other branch it runs the Chaum–Pedersen simulator with a self-chosen challenge, and the real branch challenge is fixed so that the branch challenges sum to the verifier challenge c. A proof therefore has type sigma_proto F (G × G) 2 3 2: two announcements (pairs of group elements), the challenge vector c :: [c₀; c₁] , and two responses. The prover randomness uscs consists of two commitment exponents u₁, u₂ and one simulated challenge c₁.
The overall proof._ In addition, generate_overall_proof proves, on the homomorphic product ∏ (c₁ᵢ, c₂ᵢ) of all ciphertexts of a ballot with n + 1 candidates, that it encrypts g ^ j for some 0 ≤ j ≤ n + 1, using the same disjunctive proof with n + 2 branches over the messages [g ^ 0; g ^ 1; …; g ^ (n + 1)] , proved at the index equal to the real number of approvals. This mirrors the proof Helios attaches when a question has a maximum number of approvals; here the maximum is the number of candidates.
What is proved._
  • Correctness of the encryption and projection lemmas (encryption_correct, first_project_ballot_encryption, second_project_ballot_sigma_proof).
  • Completeness: honest 0/1 votes produce accepting proofs (vote_proof_valid, ballot_proof_valid, overall_proof_valid, ballot_with_overall_proof_valid).
  • Rejection of dishonest votes generated by the honest prover algorithm on a value outside {0, 1} unless the simulated challenge equals the verifier challenge or g is the identity (vote_proof_invalid_reject, vote_proof_invalid_reject_), and the converse fact that a prover who guesses the challenge can cheat (vote_proof_invalid_accept).
  • Special soundness at the ballot level: two accepting transcripts with equal announcements and different challenges yield a witness that the ciphertext encrypts 0 or 1 (vote_proof_sound) and that the product encrypts at most the number of candidates (overall_proof_sound). Both are instances of EncProof.generalised_accepting_elgamal_soundness_main.
  • The announcements computed before the challenge is known (generate_enc_proof_commitment, generate_ballot_commitment) are exactly the announcements of the final proofs (generate_enc_proof_commitment_announcement, ballot_commitment_announcement); this justifies the Fiat–Shamir instantiation in Examples/ApprovalIns.v, which hashes these commitments to derive the challenges.
Honest-verifier zero knowledge is not re-proved in this file; it is inherited from the special HVZK of the generalised encryption proof, generalised_encryption_proof_elgamal_special_honest_verifier_zkp in Crypto/EncProof.v. Everything is parametric in an abstract field F and a vector space (group) G over it, so the development can be instantiated with Schnorr groups modulo a prime or with elliptic curves.

Ballot construction and verification

The prover and verifier algorithms. Throughout, g is the group generator, h = g ^ x the election public key, r the encryption randomness, m the vote (a field element meant to be 0 or 1), uscs the prover randomness of the Sigma proof and c the verifier challenge (in deployment obtained by Fiat–Shamir hashing).
  Section Definitions.

Single votes

encrypt_vote g h r m is the exponential ElGamal encryption (g ^ r, g ^ m · h ^ r) of the vote m with randomness r.
      Definition encrypt_vote (g h : G) (r : F) (m : F) : G × G :=
        @enc F G gop gpow g h m r.

generate_enc_proof g h r m uscs cp c is the prover side of the two-branch disjunctive encryption proof for the ciphertext cp with challenge c. If m = 0 it proves branch F1 (the real Chaum–Pedersen proof is run against the message list [g ^ m; g ^ 1] ); otherwise it proves branch FS F1 against [g ^ 0; g ^ m] . Putting m itself into the list makes the real branch consistent with the ciphertext even when m is neither 0 nor 1; such a proof is then rejected by verify_encryption_vote_proof, which checks against [g ^ 0; g ^ 1] (see vote_proof_invalid_reject).
      Definition generate_enc_proof
        (g h : G) (r : F) (m : F)
        (uscs : Vector.t F 3) (cp : G × G) (c : F) : @Sigma.sigma_proto F (G × G) 2 3 2 :=
          match Fdec m zero with
          | left _ ⇒
            @generalised_construct_encryption_proof_elgamal_real F zero add mul sub
              opp G ginv gop gpow 0 Fin.F1 r uscs [g^m; g^one] g h cp c
          | right _ ⇒
            @generalised_construct_encryption_proof_elgamal_real F zero add mul sub
              opp G ginv gop gpow 0 (Fin.FS Fin.F1) r uscs [g^zero; g^m] g h cp c
          end.

generate_enc_proof_commitment g h m uscs cp computes the two announcements of generate_enc_proof without the challenge: the real Chaum–Pedersen commitment on the proved branch and a simulated announcement on the other. A non-interactive ballot hashes these to derive the challenge (nizk_encrypt_ballot_and_generate_enc_proof_ins in Examples/ApprovalIns.v); generate_enc_proof_commitment_announcement shows they coincide with the announcements of the final proof.
      Definition generate_enc_proof_commitment
        (g h : G) (m : F) (uscs : Vector.t F 3) (cp : G × G) :
        Vector.t (G × G) 2 :=
          match Fdec m zero with
          | left _ ⇒
            @construct_encryption_proof_elgamal_commitment F opp G ginv gop gpow
              0 1 uscs [g^m; g^one] g h cp
          | right _ ⇒
            @construct_encryption_proof_elgamal_commitment F opp G ginv gop gpow
              1 0 uscs [g^zero; g^m] g h cp
          end.

encrypt_vote_and_generate_enc_proof g h r m uscs c encrypts m and attaches the disjunctive proof for the resulting ciphertext. The function is total: on a value m outside {0, 1} it still produces a proof, which the verifier rejects except with the negligible probability of a correctly guessed challenge (vote_proof_invalid_reject, vote_proof_invalid_accept).
      Definition encrypt_vote_and_generate_enc_proof
        (g h : G) (r : F) (m : F)
        (uscs : Vector.t F 3) (c : F) : G × G × @Sigma.sigma_proto F (G × G) 2 3 2 :=
        let cp := @enc F G gop gpow g h m r in
        let sig_proof := generate_enc_proof g h r m uscs cp c
        in (cp, sig_proof).

verify_encryption_vote_proof g h (cp, pf) accepts iff pf is an accepting two-branch disjunctive encryption proof that cp encrypts g ^ 0 or g ^ 1: every branch is an accepting Chaum–Pedersen conversation and the branch challenges add up to the main challenge.
      Definition verify_encryption_vote_proof (g h : G)
        (cppf : G × G × @Sigma.sigma_proto F (G × G) 2 3 2) : bool :=
        match cppf with
        | (cp, pf) ⇒ @generalised_accepting_encryption_proof_elgamal F zero add
          Fdec G ginv gop gpow Gdec _ [g^zero; g^one] g h cp pf
        end.

Whole ballots

The following functions lift the single-vote functions pointwise to vectors of length n, one entry per candidate. They are defined by recursion on n in proof mode so that they reduce by cbn.
encrypt_ballot g h r m encrypts each vote m_i with its own randomness r_i.
      Fixpoint encrypt_ballot {n : nat}
        (g h : G) (r : Vector.t F n) (m : Vector.t F n) : Vector.t (G × G) n.
      Proof.
        destruct n as [| n].
        +
          exact [].
        +
          destruct (vector_inv_S r) as (rh & rt & _).
          destruct (vector_inv_S m) as (mh & mt & _).
          exact (encrypt_vote g h rh mh :: encrypt_ballot _ g h rt mt).
      Defined.

generate_enc_proof_ballot g h r m uscs cp c produces the vote proof for each candidate, given the ciphertexts cp, the prover randomness uscs and one challenge c_i per candidate.
      Fixpoint generate_enc_proof_ballot {n : nat}
        (g h : G) (r : Vector.t F n) (m : Vector.t F n)
        (uscs : Vector.t (Vector.t F 3) n)
        (cp : Vector.t (G × G) n) (c : Vector.t F n) :
        Vector.t (@Sigma.sigma_proto F (G × G) 2 3 2) n.
      Proof.
        destruct n as [| n].
        +
          exact [].
        +
          destruct (vector_inv_S r) as (rh & rt & _).
          destruct (vector_inv_S m) as (mh & mt & _).
          destruct (vector_inv_S uscs) as (uscsh & uscst & _).
          destruct (vector_inv_S cp) as (cph & cpt & _).
          destruct (vector_inv_S c) as (ch & ct & _).
          exact(generate_enc_proof g h rh mh uscsh cph ch ::
            generate_enc_proof_ballot _ g h rt mt uscst cpt ct).
      Defined.

generate_ballot_commitment g h r m uscs computes the announcements of all the vote proofs of a ballot before any challenge is known (pointwise generate_enc_proof_commitment on the freshly computed ciphertexts).
      Fixpoint generate_ballot_commitment {n : nat}
        (g h : G) (r : Vector.t F n) (m : Vector.t F n)
        (uscs : Vector.t (Vector.t F 3) n) :
        Vector.t (Vector.t (G × G) 2) n.
      Proof.
        destruct n as [| n].
        +
          exact [].
        +
          destruct (vector_inv_S r) as (rh & rt & _).
          destruct (vector_inv_S m) as (mh & mt & _).
          destruct (vector_inv_S uscs) as (uscsh & uscst & _).
          exact (generate_enc_proof_commitment g h mh uscsh (encrypt_vote g h rh mh) ::
            generate_ballot_commitment _ g h rt mt uscst).
      Defined.

encrypt_ballot_and_generate_enc_proof g h r m uscs c produces the list of pairs (ciphertext, vote proof) for all candidates.
      Fixpoint encrypt_ballot_and_generate_enc_proof {n : nat}
        (g h : G) (r : Vector.t F n) (m : Vector.t F n)
        (uscs : Vector.t (Vector.t F 3) n) (c : Vector.t F n) :
        Vector.t (G × G × @Sigma.sigma_proto F (G × G) 2 3 2) n.
      Proof.
        destruct n as [| n].
        +
          exact [].
        +
          destruct (vector_inv_S r) as (rh & rt & _).
          destruct (vector_inv_S m) as (mh & mt & _).
          destruct (vector_inv_S uscs) as (uscsh & uscst & _).
          destruct (vector_inv_S c) as (ch & ct & _).
          exact ((encrypt_vote_and_generate_enc_proof g h rh mh uscsh ch) ::
            (encrypt_ballot_and_generate_enc_proof n
            g h rt mt uscst ct)).
      Defined.

verify_encryption_ballot_proof g h cppf is the conjunction of verify_encryption_vote_proof over all entries of the ballot.
      Fixpoint verify_encryption_ballot_proof {n : nat} (g h : G)
        (cppf : Vector.t (G × G × @Sigma.sigma_proto F (G × G) 2 3 2) n) : bool.
      Proof.
        destruct n as [| n].
        +
          exact true.
        +
          destruct (vector_inv_S cppf) as (cppfh & cppft & _).
          exact ((verify_encryption_vote_proof g h cppfh) &&
            (verify_encryption_ballot_proof _ g h cppft))%bool.
      Defined.

The overall proof

The vote proofs show that every entry encrypts 0 or 1. The overall proof additionally shows, on the homomorphic product of the ballot's ciphertexts, that the number of approvals lies between 0 and the number of candidates. It is the disjunctive encryption proof over the messages g ^ 0, …, g ^ (n + 1), proved at the index equal to the actual count. Since the per-vote proofs already bound the sum by the number of candidates, this proof is useful mainly as a template for questions with a smaller maximum.
of_nat k is the field element 1 + 1 + … + 1 (k ones).
      Fixpoint of_nat (k : nat) : F :=
        match k with
        | 0 ⇒ zero
        | S k' ⇒ one + of_nat k'
        end.

powers g k is the vector [g ^ 0; g ^ 1; …; g ^ k] of length k + 1, with g ^ (j + 1) computed as g · g ^ j.
      Fixpoint powers (g : G) (k : nat) : Vector.t G (S k) :=
        match k with
        | 0 ⇒ [g ^ zero]
        | S k' ⇒ g ^ zero :: Vector.map (fun m ⇒ gop g m) (powers g k')
        end.

ballot_product cps is the component-wise product of the ciphertexts in cps, the homomorphic encryption of the sum of the votes under the sum of the randomness (ballot_product_enc).
      Definition ballot_product {k : nat} (cps : Vector.t (G × G) k) : G × G :=
        Vector.fold_right (fun '(a, b) '(c, d) ⇒ (gop a c, gop b d)) cps (gid, gid).

count_ones ms is the number of entries of ms equal to one, that is, the number of approvals on the ballot.
      Definition count_ones {k : nat} (ms : Vector.t F k) : nat :=
        Vector.fold_right (fun m acc ⇒ if Fdec m one then S acc else acc) ms 0.

The number of approvals is bounded by the number of candidates.
      Lemma count_ones_le : ∀ (k : nat) (ms : Vector.t F k), count_ones ms ≤ k.
      Proof.
        induction k as [| k ih]; intros ms.
        + rewrite (vector_inv_0 ms); cbn; lia.
        + destruct (vector_inv_S ms) as (m & ms' & hm); subst.
          unfold count_ones in *; cbn.
          destruct (Fdec m one); specialize (ih ms'); lia.
      Qed.

overall_index ms is the branch index count_ones ms of the overall proof, as an element of Fin.t (2 + n) (the bound comes from count_ones_le).
      Definition overall_index {n : nat} (ms : Vector.t F (S n)) : Fin.t (2 + n) :=
        Fin.of_nat_lt (le_n_S _ _ (count_ones_le _ ms)).

generate_overall_proof g h rs ms uscs c proves that the product of the ciphertexts of the ballot ms (with randomness rs) encrypts g ^ (count_ones ms), using the (n + 2)-branch disjunctive proof over powers g (S n) with real branch overall_index ms and witness Σ rs. The prover randomness uscs holds n + 2 commitment exponents and n + 1 simulated challenges.
      Definition generate_overall_proof {n : nat} (g h : G)
        (rs ms : Vector.t F (S n)) (uscs : Vector.t F ((2 + n) + (1 + n))) (c : F) :
        @Sigma.sigma_proto F (G × G) (2 + n) (1 + (2 + n)) (2 + n) :=
        @generalised_construct_encryption_proof_elgamal_real F zero add mul sub opp
          G ginv gop gpow n (overall_index ms) (Vector.fold_right add rs zero)
          uscs (powers g (S n)) g h (ballot_product (encrypt_ballot g h rs ms)) c.

verify_overall_proof g h cps pf checks the overall proof against the product of cps and the message list powers g k. A ballot with no candidates (k = 0) has nothing to prove and is accepted; the dependent match is needed because the proof type depends on k.
      Definition verify_overall_proof {k : nat} (g h : G) (cps : Vector.t (G × G) k)
        (pf : @Sigma.sigma_proto F (G × G) (S k) (S (S k)) (S k)) : bool :=
        match k as k' return Vector.t (G × G) k' →
          @Sigma.sigma_proto F (G × G) (S k') (S (S k')) (S k') → bool
        with
        | 0 ⇒ fun _ _ ⇒ true
        | S k' ⇒ fun cps pf ⇒
          @generalised_accepting_encryption_proof_elgamal F zero add Fdec
            G ginv gop gpow Gdec (S (S k')) (powers g (S k')) g h (ballot_product cps) pf
        end cps pf.

encrypt_ballot_with_overall_proof produces the full ballot: one ciphertext and vote proof per candidate, together with the overall proof on their product.
      Definition encrypt_ballot_with_overall_proof {n : nat} (g h : G)
        (rs ms : Vector.t F (S n)) (uscs : Vector.t (Vector.t F 3) (S n))
        (cs : Vector.t F (S n)) (uscs' : Vector.t F ((2 + n) + (1 + n))) (c : F) :
        Vector.t (G × G × @Sigma.sigma_proto F (G × G) 2 3 2) (S n) ×
        @Sigma.sigma_proto F (G × G) (2 + n) (1 + (2 + n)) (2 + n) :=
        (encrypt_ballot_and_generate_enc_proof g h rs ms uscs cs,
         generate_overall_proof g h rs ms uscs' c).

verify_ballot g h b accepts a full ballot iff all vote proofs and the overall proof (computed on the ciphertexts contained in b) are accepting.
      Definition verify_ballot {k : nat} (g h : G)
        (b : Vector.t (G × G × @Sigma.sigma_proto F (G × G) 2 3 2) k ×
             @Sigma.sigma_proto F (G × G) (S k) (S (S k)) (S k)) : bool :=
        (verify_encryption_ballot_proof g h (fst b) &&
         verify_overall_proof g h (Vector.map fst (fst b)) (snd b))%bool.

    End Definitions.

Projections and encryption correctness

The ciphertext component of encrypt_vote_and_generate_enc_proof is encrypt_vote.
      Theorem first_projection_vote_encryption :
        ∀ (r : F) (g h : G) (m : F)
        (uscs : Vector.t F 3) (c : F),
        fst (encrypt_vote_and_generate_enc_proof g h r m uscs c) =
        encrypt_vote g h r m.
      Proof.
        intros *;
        unfold encrypt_vote_and_generate_enc_proof,
        enc, encrypt_vote, enc; cbn.
        reflexivity.
      Qed.

The proof component of encrypt_vote_and_generate_enc_proof is generate_enc_proof applied to the ciphertext cp, provided cp is the encryption of m with randomness r.
      Theorem second_projection_vote_sigma_proof :
        ∀ (r : F) (g h : G) (m : F)
        (uscs : Vector.t F 3) (cp : G × G) (c : F),
        cp = @enc F G gop gpow g h m r →
        snd (encrypt_vote_and_generate_enc_proof g h r m uscs c) =
        generate_enc_proof g h r m uscs cp c.
      Proof.
        intros × ha.
        unfold encrypt_vote_and_generate_enc_proof,
        generate_enc_proof; subst. simpl.
        reflexivity.
      Qed.

Ballot-level version of first_projection_vote_encryption: the ciphertexts of an encrypted-and-proved ballot are encrypt_ballot g h r m.
      Theorem first_project_ballot_encryption :
        ∀ (n : nat) (r : Vector.t F n) (g h : G) (m : Vector.t F n)
          (uscs : Vector.t (Vector.t F 3) n) (c : Vector.t F n),
        Vector.map fst (encrypt_ballot_and_generate_enc_proof g h r m uscs c) =
        encrypt_ballot g h r m.
      Proof.
        induction n as [|n ihn].
        +
          intros ×.
          reflexivity.
        +
          intros ×.
          destruct (vector_inv_S r) as (rh & rt & ha).
          destruct (vector_inv_S m) as (mh & mt & hb).
          destruct (vector_inv_S uscs) as (uscsh & uscst & hc).
          destruct (vector_inv_S c) as (ch & ct & hd).
          subst. cbn.
          rewrite ihn.
          unfold encrypt_vote.
          reflexivity.
      Qed.

Ballot-level version of second_projection_vote_sigma_proof: if every cp_i encrypts m_i with r_i, the proofs of the ballot are generate_enc_proof_ballot g h r m uscs cp c.

        ∀ (n : nat) (r : Vector.t F n) (g h : G) (m : Vector.t F n)
        (uscs : Vector.t (Vector.t F 3) n) (cp : Vector.t (G × G) n) (c : Vector.t F n),
        (∀ (i : Fin.t n), Vector.nth cp i = @enc F G gop gpow g h (Vector.nth m i) (Vector.nth r i)) →
        Vector.map snd (encrypt_ballot_and_generate_enc_proof g h r m uscs c) =
        generate_enc_proof_ballot g h r m uscs cp c.
      Proof.
        induction n as [|n ihn].
        +
          intros × ha.
          reflexivity.
        +
          intros × hf.
          destruct (vector_inv_S r) as (rh & rt & ha).
          destruct (vector_inv_S m) as (mh & mt & hb).
          destruct (vector_inv_S uscs) as (uscsh & uscst & hc).
          destruct (vector_inv_S cp) as (cph & cpt & hd).
          destruct (vector_inv_S c) as (ch & ct & he).
          subst. cbn. f_equal.
          specialize (hf Fin.F1); cbn in hf.
          subst. reflexivity.
          eapply ihn. intro f.
          specialize (hf (Fin.FS f)).
          cbn in hf. exact hf.
      Qed.

Completeness and behaviour on invalid votes

Completeness of the vote proof: if m = 0 or m = 1, the honestly generated proof is accepted for every choice of prover randomness and challenge. The proof applies the completeness of the generalised encryption proof (generalised_construct_encryption_proof_elgamal_real_completeness) and checks that the real branch statement c₂ · (g ^ m)⁻¹ = h ^ r holds.
      Theorem vote_proof_valid :
        ∀ (r : F) (g h : G) (m : F) (uscs : Vector.t F 3) (c : F),
          (m = zero ∨ m = one) →
          verify_encryption_vote_proof g h
            (encrypt_vote_and_generate_enc_proof g h r m uscs c) = true.
      Proof.
        intros × ha.
        unfold encrypt_vote_and_generate_enc_proof,
        verify_encryption_vote_proof, generate_enc_proof.
        destruct (Fdec m zero) as [fa | fa].
        +
          subst.
          eapply generalised_construct_encryption_proof_elgamal_real_completeness.
          subst; cbn. split. reflexivity.
          assert (Hwt : gop (g ^ zero) (h ^ r) = gop (h ^ r) (g ^ zero)).
          rewrite commutative; reflexivity.
          rewrite Hwt; clear Hwt.
          setoid_rewrite <-(@monoid_is_associative G (@eq G) gop gid).
          assert (Hwt : (gop (g ^ zero) (gop (ginv (g ^ zero)) (h ^ r))) =
          (h ^ r)).
          rewrite associative.
          rewrite group_is_right_inverse,
          monoid_is_left_idenity;
          reflexivity. rewrite associative, commutative in Hwt.
          rewrite Hwt. reflexivity.
          typeclasses eauto.
        +
          destruct ha as [ha | ha]; try congruence. subst.
          eapply generalised_construct_encryption_proof_elgamal_real_completeness.
          split. reflexivity.
          cbn. subst.
          assert (Hwt : gop (g ^ one) (h ^ r) = gop (h ^ r) (g ^ one)).
          rewrite commutative; reflexivity.
          rewrite Hwt; clear Hwt.
          setoid_rewrite <-(@monoid_is_associative G (@eq G) gop gid).
          assert (Hwt : (gop (g ^ one) (gop (ginv (g ^ one)) (h ^ r))) =
          (h ^ r)).
          rewrite associative.
          rewrite group_is_right_inverse,
          monoid_is_left_idenity;
          reflexivity. rewrite associative, commutative in Hwt.
          rewrite Hwt. reflexivity.
          typeclasses eauto.
      Qed.

A field has no zero divisors: a · b = 0 → a = 0 ∨ b = 0.
      Lemma mul_eq_zero (a b : F) : a × b = zero → a = zero ∨ b = zero.
      Proof.
        intro ha.
        destruct (Fdec a zero) as [hb | hb].
        -
          left; assumption.
        -
          right.
          eapply f_equal with (f := fun x ⇒ inv a × x) in ha.
          rewrite associative in ha.
          assert (hc : inv a × a = one). field.
          assumption.
          rewrite hc in ha; clear hc.
          assert (hc : inv a × zero = zero). field.
          assumption. rewrite hc in ha; clear hc.
          assert (hc : one × b = b). field.
          rewrite hc in ha. exact ha.
      Qed.

u - v = 0 → u = v.

      Proof.

        rewrite hb in ha.

        rewrite hc in ha; clear hc.
        assert (hc : u + zero = u) by field.
        rewrite hc in ha; clear hc.
        assert (hc : zero + v = v) by field.
        rewrite hc in ha. exact ha.
      Qed.

Rejection of invalid votes: if g ≠ 1, m ∉ {0, 1} and the simulated challenge c₁ differs from the verifier challenge c, then the honestly generated proof for m is rejected.
Proof idea: the verifier checks the second branch against g ^ 1 while the prover produced it for g ^ m. The second branch gets challenge c - c₁; unfolding its Chaum–Pedersen check reduces acceptance to g ^ ((m - 1) · (c - c₁)) = 1. Since g ≠ 1, the exponent is zero, and the absence of zero divisors gives m = 1 or c = c₁, both excluded.

The i-th entry of powers g k is g ^ (of_nat i).

Rearrangement in the commutative group: (a · b) · (c · d) = (a · c) · (b · d).
      Lemma gop_rearrange : ∀ (a b c d : G),
        gop (gop a b) (gop c d) = gop (gop a c) (gop b d).
      Proof.
        intros a b c d.
        rewrite <- associative, (associative b c d), (commutative b c),
          <- (associative c b d), associative.
        reflexivity.
      Qed.

Homomorphism: the product of the encryptions of ms with randomness rs is the encryption of Σ ms with randomness Σ rs.
      Lemma ballot_product_enc : ∀ (k : nat) (g h : G) (rs ms : Vector.t F k),
        ballot_product (encrypt_ballot g h rs ms) =
        (g ^ Vector.fold_right add rs zero,
         gop (g ^ Vector.fold_right add ms zero) (h ^ Vector.fold_right add rs zero)).
      Proof.
        induction k as [| k ih]; intros g h rs ms.
        + rewrite (vector_inv_0 rs), (vector_inv_0 ms); cbn.
          rewrite !vector_space_field_zero, left_identity; reflexivity.
        + destruct (vector_inv_S rs) as (r & rs' & hr).
          destruct (vector_inv_S ms) as (m & ms' & hm).

            (encrypt_ballot g h rs' ms') (gid, gid))
            with (ballot_product (encrypt_ballot g h rs' ms')).

          all: typeclasses eauto.
      Qed.

Special soundness of the vote proof: from two accepting transcripts for the ciphertext cp with the same announcement a and different main challenges c₁ ≠ c₂, one extracts r such that cp encrypts 0 or 1 with randomness r. Instance of generalised_accepting_elgamal_soundness_main with two branches; the extracted equation c₂ · (g ^ m)⁻¹ = h ^ r is rearranged to the ciphertext form.
      Theorem vote_proof_sound : ∀ (g h : G) (cp : G × G)
        (a : Vector.t (G × G) 2) (c₁ c₂ : F) (cs₁ cs₂ rs₁ rs₂ : Vector.t F 2),
        verify_encryption_vote_proof g h (cp, (a; c₁ :: cs₁; rs₁)) = true →
        verify_encryption_vote_proof g h (cp, (a; c₂ :: cs₂; rs₂)) = true →
        c₁ ≠ c₂ →
        ∃ (r : F), cp = (g ^ r, gop (g ^ zero) (h ^ r)) ∨
          cp = (g ^ r, gop (g ^ one) (h ^ r)).
      Proof.
        intros × ha hb hc.
        unfold verify_encryption_vote_proof in ha, hb.
        destruct cp as (p₁, p₂).
        destruct (generalised_accepting_elgamal_soundness_main (n := 0) g h
          [g ^ zero; g ^ one] p₁ p₂ a c₁ cs₁ rs₁ c₂ cs₂ rs₂ ha hb hc) as
          (y & hy & f & hf).
        assert (hg : ∀ (p w v : G), v = gop p (ginv w) → p = gop w v).
        { intros × hv; rewrite hv, commutative, <-associative,
            group_is_left_inverse, monoid_is_right_identity; reflexivity. }
        ∃ y.
        destruct (fin_inv_S _ f) as [hf0 | (f' & hf1)]; subst.
        + left. cbn in hf. f_equal; eapply hg; exact hf.
        + destruct (fin_inv_S _ f') as [hf0 | (f'' & hf1)]; subst;
          [| refine match f'' with end].
          right. cbn in hf. f_equal; eapply hg; exact hf.
      Qed.

Special soundness of the overall proof: two accepting overall proofs for the same ciphertexts cps (of k + 1 candidates) with equal announcement and different challenges yield r and a count m ≤ k + 1 such that the product of cps equals (g ^ r, g ^ (of_nat m) · h ^ r), that is, the ballot carries at most k + 1 approvals.
      Theorem overall_proof_sound : ∀ (k : nat) (g h : G)
        (cps : Vector.t (G × G) (S k))
        (a : Vector.t (G × G) (S (S k))) (c₁ c₂ : F)
        (cs₁ cs₂ rs₁ rs₂ : Vector.t F (S (S k))),
        verify_overall_proof g h cps (a; c₁ :: cs₁; rs₁) = true →
        verify_overall_proof g h cps (a; c₂ :: cs₂; rs₂) = true →
        c₁ ≠ c₂ →
        ∃ (r : F) (m : nat), m ≤ S k ∧
          ballot_product cps = (g ^ r, gop (g ^ of_nat m) (h ^ r)).
      Proof.
        intros × ha hb hc.
        unfold verify_overall_proof in ha, hb.
        destruct (ballot_product cps) as (p₁, p₂) eqn:hp.
        destruct (generalised_accepting_elgamal_soundness_main (n := k) g h
          (powers g (S k)) p₁ p₂ a c₁ cs₁ rs₁ c₂ cs₂ rs₂ ha hb hc) as
