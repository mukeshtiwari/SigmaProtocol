The IVXV campaign group

The 2048-bit safe-prime group used in the IVXV (Estonian internet voting) benchmark campaign: the group of bench/ivxv-diff/group.txt, produced by Verificatum's vog -gen ModPGroup -fixed 2048. Here p = 2q + 1 with q of 2047 bits, and the group is the order-q subgroup of Z_p^* (Utility/Zpstar.v) with the generator g of the group description.
The primality of p and q is ASSUMED, not certified: prime_q and prime_p are axioms, and therefore show up in Print Assumptions of everything built on this file. The relation p = k·q + 1 (with k = p / q = 2) and the order of g are checked by computation.
The file is generated by bench/ivxv-diff/mkins.py from the group part of HeliosTallyIns.v and should not be edited by hand. It is used by VerificatumIvxvIns.v and extracted to OCaml by src/Extraction/Ivxvlib.

gval^q mod p = 1, so gval lies in the subgroup of order q. The power is computed with the BigN implementation of BigNPow, proved equal to Zpow_mod.
  Theorem g_order : Zpow_mod gval q p = 1%Z.
  Proof.
    unfold q; rewrite BigNPow.bpow_mod_spec by (vm_compute; discriminate).
    vm_cast_no_check (eq_refl 1%Z).
  Qed.
