Helios tally verifier at the IACR election parameters

This file instantiates the Helios tally certificate of Backend/HeliosTally.v at the group used by the IACR elections run on Helios (vote.heliosvoting.org, 2022 to 2024). The group is the order-q subgroup of Z_p^*, where p is a 2048-bit prime and q a 256-bit prime with p = k q + 1, generated by g. The three elections share p, q and g and differ only in the election public key h. The keys of the 2024 election (7 candidates) and the 2023 election (6 candidates) are included.
Primality of p and q is not recomputed here. It is imported from the certificates in Examples/primeP.v and Examples/primeQ.v. Subgroup membership of g and of each h (x^q mod p = 1) is checked by vm_compute with vm_cast_no_check.
The only computational definition is compute_final_count_ins, the correct-by-construction tally verifier. It is extracted by src/Extraction/HeliosTallylib/Extraction.v (together with Examples/HeliosFrontendIns.v) and by src/Extraction/Verificatumlib/Extraction.v. It is run on the published election data by src/Executable/HeliosVerifiercode, and is also used by src/Executable/HeliosBenchcode and src/Executable/HeliosJscode. The same group is reused by Examples/SchulzeBenchIns.v and the WebAssembly benchmarks.

The Helios tally verifier at this group, for an election with public key h, n answers and 1 + m trustees. Given the published ballots bs, the trustees' data ts (decryption factors, proofs and public keys) and the claimed plaintext tally pt, it returns the verdict, the valid and invalid ballots, and a count derivation that justifies the verdict (HeliosTally.compute_final_count).
  Definition compute_final_count_ins {n m : nat} (h : @Schnorr_group p q)
    (bs : list (@ballot (@Zp q) (@Schnorr_group p q) n))
    (ts : Vector.t (@tallier (@Zp q) (@Schnorr_group p q) n) (1 + m))
    (pt : Vector.t (@Zp q) n) :
    existsT (bfinal : bool)
    (vbs inbs : list (@ballot (@Zp q) (@Schnorr_group p q) n)),
    @count (@Zp q)
    (@Zpfield.zero q prime_q)
    (@Zpfield.one q prime_q) zp_add zp_dec
    (@Schnorr_group p q) (@Schnorr.one p q prime_p prime_q)
    (@inv_schnorr_group _ p q safe_prime prime_p prime_q)
    (@mul_schnorr_group p q prime_p prime_q)
    (@pow k p q safe_prime prime_p prime_q)
    Schnorr.dec_zpstar n m g h
    (finished bs vbs inbs ts pt bfinal).
  Proof.
    refine(@compute_final_count (@Zp q)
    (@Zpfield.zero q prime_q)
    (@Zpfield.one q prime_q) zp_add zp_dec
    (@Schnorr_group p q) (@Schnorr.one p q prime_p prime_q)
    (@inv_schnorr_group _ p q safe_prime prime_p prime_q)
    (@mul_schnorr_group p q prime_p prime_q)
    (@pow k p q safe_prime prime_p prime_q)
    Schnorr.dec_zpstar n m g h bs ts pt).
  Defined.
