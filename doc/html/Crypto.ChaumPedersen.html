The Chaum–Pedersen Protocol

This file instantiates the generic equality-of-discrete-logarithms protocol of EqSigma at two bases, giving the Chaum–Pedersen protocol (Chaum and Pedersen, ‘Wallet databases with observers’, Crypto 1992). For public group elements g, h, c₁, c₂ the prover shows knowledge of x with

      g ^ x = c₁ ∧ h ^ x = c₂
i.e. that (g, h, c₁, c₂) is a Diffie–Hellman tuple. Typical uses: x is ElGamal encryption randomness (so c₁ = g ^ x is the first ciphertext component and c₂ = h ^ x the mask under public key h), or x is a secret key and the protocol proves correct partial decryption.
Protocol, with randomness u and challenge c:

      announcement (g ^ u, h ^ u)
      response r = u + c · x
      check g ^ r = a₁ · c₁ ^ c and h ^ r = a₂ · c₂ ^ c
Every definition here is a thin wrapper that fixes gs = [g; h] and hs = [c₁; c₂] in the corresponding EqSigma definition, and every property is inherited from the EqSigma results: completeness, special soundness (two accepting transcripts with distinct challenges yield the witness), and special honest-verifier zero-knowledge (both the weak verdict-level form and the strong ‘equal distributions’ form). The file also gives the verifier in explicit two-equation form (generalised_cp_accepting_conversations_accept and generalised_cp_accepting_conversations_reject), which is convenient for the extracted verifiers in Backend.

Definitions

    Section Def.
The announcement vector, with entries g ^ u and h ^ u, for randomness u.
      Definition construct_cp_conversations_schnorr_commitment
         (g h : G) (u : F) : Vector.t G 2 :=
        @construct_eq_conversations_schnorr_commitment
          F G gpow _ [g; h] u.

Honest prover transcript ([g ^ u; h ^ u]; [c]; [u + c · x]) for witness x, randomness u and challenge c.
      Definition construct_cp_conversations_schnorr
        (x : F) (g h : G) (u c : F) : @sigma_proto F G 2 1 1 :=
        @construct_eq_conversations_schnorr F add mul G gpow _ x
          [g; h] u c.

Simulator: for a response u and challenge c it outputs announcements g ^ u · c₁ ^ (-c) and h ^ u · c₂ ^ (-c), so the transcript is accepting without knowledge of x.
      Definition construct_cp_conversations_simulator
        (g h : G) (c₁ c₂ : G) (u c : F) : @sigma_proto F G 2 1 1 :=
        @construct_eq_conversations_simulator F opp G gop gpow _
        [g; h] [c₁; c₂] u c.

Verifier: accepts ([a₁; a₂]; [c]; [r]) iff g ^ r = a₁ · c₁ ^ c and h ^ r = a₂ · c₂ ^ c.
      Definition generalised_cp_accepting_conversations (g h : G) (c₁ c₂ : G)
        (s : @sigma_proto F G 2 1 1) : bool :=
        @generalised_eq_accepting_conversations F G gop gpow Gdec _ [g; h]
        [c₁; c₂] s.

Distribution of honest transcripts for challenge c, with u drawn uniformly from the non-empty list lf.
      Definition generalised_cp_schnorr_distribution
        (lf : list F) (Hlfn : lf ≠ List.nil) (x : F)
        (g h : G) (c : F) : dist (@sigma_proto F G 2 1 1) :=
        @generalised_eq_schnorr_distribution F add mul G gpow _
          lf Hlfn x [g; h] c.

Distribution of simulated transcripts for challenge c, with the response drawn uniformly from lf.
      Definition generalised_cp_simulator_distribution
        (lf : list F) (Hlfn : lf ≠ List.nil) (g h : G)
        (c₁ c₂ : G) c : dist (@sigma_proto F G 2 1 1) :=
        @generalised_eq_simulator_distribution F opp G gop
        gpow _ lf Hlfn [g; h] [c₁; c₂] c.

    End Def.

Proofs

The first five theorems characterise the boolean verifier by its two group equations; they need no algebraic hypotheses.
    Section Proofs.

Acceptance implies both verification equations.
      Theorem generalised_cp_accepting_conversations_accept_forward :
        ∀ (g h c₁ c₂ : G) (a₁ a₂ : G) (c r : F),
        generalised_cp_accepting_conversations g h c₁ c₂
        ([a₁; a₂]; [c]; [r]) = true → g ^ r = gop a₁ (c₁ ^ c) ∧
        h ^ r = gop a₂ (c₂ ^ c).
      Proof.
        intros × ha. unfold generalised_cp_accepting_conversations,
        generalised_eq_accepting_conversations. cbn in ha.
        eapply andb_true_iff in ha.
        destruct ha as (hal & har).
        rewrite dec_true in hal, har.
        rewrite hal, har; split; reflexivity.
      Qed.

Both verification equations imply acceptance.
      Theorem generalised_cp_accepting_conversations_accept_backward :
        ∀ (g h c₁ c₂ : G) (a₁ a₂ : G) (c r : F),
        g ^ r = gop a₁ (c₁ ^ c) → h ^ r = gop a₂ (c₂ ^ c) →
        generalised_cp_accepting_conversations g h c₁ c₂
        ([a₁; a₂]; [c]; [r]) = true.
      Proof.
        intros × ha hb.
        cbn. eapply andb_true_iff; split;
        eapply dec_true; [exact ha | exact hb].
      Qed.

Acceptance is equivalent to g ^ r = a₁ · c₁ ^ c ∧ h ^ r = a₂ · c₂ ^ c.
      Theorem generalised_cp_accepting_conversations_accept :
        ∀ (g h c₁ c₂ : G) (a₁ a₂ : G) (c r : F),
        generalised_cp_accepting_conversations g h c₁ c₂
        ([a₁; a₂]; [c]; [r]) = true ↔ g ^ r = gop a₁ (c₁ ^ c) ∧
        h ^ r = gop a₂ (c₂ ^ c).
      Proof.
        intros *; split; intros ha.
        eapply generalised_cp_accepting_conversations_accept_forward;
        assumption.
        eapply generalised_cp_accepting_conversations_accept_backward;
        destruct ha as (hal & har); assumption.
      Qed.

Rejection implies that at least one verification equation fails.
      Theorem generalised_cp_accepting_conversations_reject_forward :
        ∀ (g h c₁ c₂ : G) (a₁ a₂ : G) (c r : F),
        generalised_cp_accepting_conversations g h c₁ c₂
        ([a₁; a₂]; [c]; [r]) = false → g ^ r ≠ gop a₁ (c₁ ^ c) ∨
        h ^ r ≠ gop a₂ (c₂ ^ c).
      Proof.
        intros × ha; cbn in ha.
        eapply andb_false_iff in ha.
        destruct ha as [ha | ha].
        +
          eapply dec_false in ha;
          left; exact ha.
        +
          eapply dec_false in ha;
          right; exact ha.
      Qed.

If at least one verification equation fails, the verifier rejects.
      Theorem generalised_cp_accepting_conversations_reject_backward :
        ∀ (g h c₁ c₂ : G) (a₁ a₂ : G) (c r : F),
        (g ^ r ≠ gop a₁ (c₁ ^ c) ∨ h ^ r ≠ gop a₂ (c₂ ^ c)) →
        generalised_cp_accepting_conversations g h c₁ c₂
        ([a₁; a₂]; [c]; [r]) = false.
      Proof.
        intros × [ha | ha].
        +
          cbn. eapply andb_false_iff.
          left. eapply dec_false.
          exact ha.
        +
          cbn. eapply andb_false_iff.
          right. eapply dec_false.
          exact ha.
      Qed.

Rejection is equivalent to the failure of at least one verification equation.
      Theorem generalised_cp_accepting_conversations_reject :
        ∀ (g h c₁ c₂ : G) (a₁ a₂ : G) (c r : F),
        (g ^ r ≠ gop a₁ (c₁ ^ c) ∨ h ^ r ≠ gop a₂ (c₂ ^ c)) ↔
        generalised_cp_accepting_conversations g h c₁ c₂
        ([a₁; a₂]; [c]; [r]) = false.
      Proof.
        intros *; split; intros ha.
        eapply generalised_cp_accepting_conversations_reject_backward;
        assumption.
        eapply generalised_cp_accepting_conversations_reject_forward;
        assumption.
      Qed.

Completeness: if g ^ x = c₁ and h ^ x = c₂, the honest transcript is accepted for every u and c. Instance of construct_eq_conversations_schnorr_completeness; the proof discharges the index-wise relation by case analysis on Fin.t 2.
      Lemma construct_cp_conversations_schnorr_completeness
        (x : F)
        (g h c₁ c₂ : G)
        (R : g^x = c₁ ∧ h^x = c₂) :
        ∀ (u c : F),
        generalised_cp_accepting_conversations g h c₁ c₂
          (construct_cp_conversations_schnorr x g h u c) = true.
      Proof.
        intros ×. destruct R as (Ra & Rb).
        eapply construct_eq_conversations_schnorr_completeness.
        intro f.
        destruct (fin_inv_S _ f) as [ea | (fi & ea)].
        subst; cbn. exact eq_refl.
        destruct (fin_inv_S _ fi) as [eaa | (fii & eaa)].
        subst; cbn. exact eq_refl.
        refine match (fin_inv_0 fii) with end.
      Qed.

Simulator completeness: simulated transcripts are always accepted, for any g, h, c₁, c₂.
      Lemma construct_cp_conversations_simulator_completeness
        (g h c₁ c₂ : G) :
        ∀ (u c : F),
        generalised_cp_accepting_conversations g h c₁ c₂
          (construct_cp_conversations_simulator g h c₁ c₂ u c) = true.
      Proof.
        intros ×.
        eapply construct_eq_conversations_simulator_completeness.
      Qed.

Special soundness: two accepting transcripts with the same announcement and distinct challenges cr₁ ≠ cr₂ yield y with g ^ y = c₁ and h ^ y = c₂ (stated index-wise over Fin.t 2). Instance of generalise_eq_sigma_soundness.
      Lemma generalise_cp_sigma_soundness
        (g h c₁ c₂ : G) :
        ∀ (a : Vector.t G 2)
        (cr₁ cr₂ : F) (r₁ r₂ : F),
        generalised_cp_accepting_conversations g h c₁ c₂ (a; [cr₁]; [r₁]) = true →
        generalised_cp_accepting_conversations g h c₁ c₂ (a; [cr₂]; [r₂]) = true →
        cr₁ ≠ cr₂ →
        ∃ y : F, (∀ (f : Fin.t 2),
          (nth [g; h] f)^y = (nth [c₁; c₂] f)).
      Proof.
        intros × ha hb hc.
        eapply generalise_eq_sigma_soundness
        with (a := a) (c₁ := cr₁)
        (c₂ := cr₂) (r₁ := r₁) (r₂ := r₂).
        exact ha. exact hb. exact hc.
      Qed.

Special honest-verifier zero-knowledge, weak form: under the relation, the honest and simulated distributions coincide after mapping each transcript to the verifier’s verdict (every entry is (true, 1 / |lf|)).
      Lemma generalised_cp_special_honest_verifier_zkp
        (x : F)
        (g h c₁ c₂ : G)
        (R : g^x = c₁ ∧ h^x = c₂) :
        ∀ (lf : list F) (Hlfn : lf ≠ List.nil) (c : F),
        List.map (fun '(a, p) ⇒
          (generalised_cp_accepting_conversations g h c₁ c₂ a, p))
          (@generalised_cp_schnorr_distribution lf Hlfn x g h c) =
        List.map (fun '(a, p) ⇒
          (generalised_cp_accepting_conversations g h c₁ c₂ a, p))
          (@generalised_cp_simulator_distribution lf Hlfn g h c₁ c₂ c).
      Proof.
        intros ×. destruct R as (Ra & Rb).
        eapply generalised_eq_special_honest_verifier_zkp.
        intro f.
        destruct (fin_inv_S _ f) as [ea | (fi & ea)].
        subst; cbn. exact eq_refl.
        destruct (fin_inv_S _ fi) as [eaa | (fii & eaa)].
        subst; cbn. exact eq_refl.
        refine match (fin_inv_0 fii) with end.
      Qed.

Special honest-verifier zero-knowledge, strong form: under the relation, if u ↦ u + c · x permutes lf, then the honest and simulated transcript distributions are permutations of each other. Instance of generalised_eq_special_honest_verifier_zkp_perm.
      Theorem generalised_cp_special_honest_verifier_zkp_perm
        (x : F) (g h c₁ c₂ : G) (R : g^x = c₁ ∧ h^x = c₂) :
        ∀ (lf : list F) (Hlfn : lf ≠ List.nil) (c : F),
        Permutation (List.map (fun u ⇒ u + c × x) lf) lf →
        Permutation
          (@generalised_cp_schnorr_distribution lf Hlfn x g h c)
          (@generalised_cp_simulator_distribution lf Hlfn g h c₁ c₂ c).
      Proof.
        intros × hp; destruct R as (Ra & Rb).
        unfold generalised_cp_schnorr_distribution,
          generalised_cp_simulator_distribution.
        eapply generalised_eq_special_honest_verifier_zkp_perm; [| exact hp].
        intro f.
        destruct (fin_inv_S _ f) as [ea | (fi & ea)]; subst; cbn; [reflexivity |].
        destruct (fin_inv_S _ fi) as [eb | (fj & eb)]; subst; cbn; [reflexivity |].
        refine (match fj with end).
      Qed.

Special honest-verifier zero-knowledge when lf enumerates the field without duplicates: the honest and simulated transcript distributions are permutations of each other.
      Theorem generalised_cp_special_honest_verifier_zkp_enum
        (x : F) (g h c₁ c₂ : G) (R : g^x = c₁ ∧ h^x = c₂) :
        ∀ (lf : list F) (Hlfn : lf ≠ List.nil) (c : F),
        List.NoDup lf → (∀ y : F, List.In y lf) →
        Permutation
          (@generalised_cp_schnorr_distribution lf Hlfn x g h c)
          (@generalised_cp_simulator_distribution lf Hlfn g h c₁ c₂ c).
      Proof.
        intros × hnd hall.
        eapply generalised_cp_special_honest_verifier_zkp_perm; [exact R |].
        eapply enumerates_perm_map with (psi := fun u ⇒ u - c × x);
        [exact hnd | exact hall | intros u; field | intros u; field].
      Qed.

    End Proofs.
  End CP.
