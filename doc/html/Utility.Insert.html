Library Utility.Insert

Inserting into and removing from length-indexed vectors

Utilities for Vector.t A k indexed by Fin.t: insert_at k v i a places a at position i of v, producing a vector of length S k, and remove_at is its inverse. They are used by the generalised OR-composition of Sigma protocols (Crypto/OrSigmaGen.v) and by Crypto/EncProof.v, where the challenge for the real branch is inserted at the secret index i among the simulated challenges, so that all challenges sum to the verifier's challenge.
Besides the round-trip lemmas (remove_insert, insert_remove) the file contains transport lemmas for rew along equalities of lengths (rew_cons, rew_FS, rew_app_cons), using uniqueness of identity proofs on nat (UIP_nat, which holds without axioms because equality on nat is decidable), and compatibility of zip_with with insertion and concatenation.

Insertion and removal

Section Insert.
The element type.
  Context {A : Type}.

insert_at k v i a: the vector of length S k that has a at index i and the entries of v, in order, at the other indices. Defined by recursion on k and case analysis on i (Fin.caseS'); inserting at F1 conses.
  Fixpoint insert_at (k : nat) : Vector.t A k → Fin.t (S k) → A → Vector.t A (S k) :=
    match k with
    | 0 ⇒ fun v i a ⇒ [a]
    | S k' ⇒ fun v i a ⇒
        Fin.caseS' i (fun _ ⇒ Vector.t A (S (S k')))
          (a :: v) (fun j ⇒ hd v :: insert_at k' (tl v) j a)
    end.

remove_at k v i: delete the entry at index i from a vector of length S k.
  Fixpoint remove_at (k : nat) : Vector.t A (S k) → Fin.t (S k) → Vector.t A k :=
    match k with
    | 0 ⇒ fun v i ⇒ []
    | S k' ⇒ fun v i ⇒
        Fin.caseS' i (fun _ ⇒ Vector.t A (S k'))
          (tl v) (fun j ⇒ hd v :: remove_at k' (tl v) j)
    end.

Removing what was just inserted gives back the original vector.
  Lemma remove_insert : ∀ k (v : Vector.t A k) (i : Fin.t (S k)) (a : A),
    remove_at k (insert_at k v i a) i = v.
  Proof.
    induction k as [| k ih]; intros v i a.
    + rewrite (vector_inv_0 v); reflexivity.
    + destruct (fin_inv_S _ i) as [hi | (j & hi)]; subst; cbn.
      × reflexivity.
      × destruct (vector_inv_S v) as (b & w & hv); subst; cbn.
        f_equal; apply ih.
  Qed.

Re-inserting the removed entry gives back the original vector.
  Lemma insert_remove : ∀ k (v : Vector.t A (S k)) (i : Fin.t (S k)),
    insert_at k (remove_at k v i) i (Vector.nth v i) = v.
  Proof.
    induction k as [| k ih]; intros v i.
    + destruct (vector_inv_S v) as (b & w & hv);
      rewrite (vector_inv_0 w) in hv; subst.
      destruct (fin_inv_S _ i) as [hi | (j & hi)]; subst;
      [reflexivity | inversion j].
    + destruct (vector_inv_S v) as (b & w & hv); subst.
      destruct (fin_inv_S _ i) as [hi | (j & hi)]; subst; cbn.
      × reflexivity.
      × f_equal; apply ih.
  Qed.

The inserted entry is found at the insertion index.
  Lemma nth_insert_at : ∀ k (v : Vector.t A k) (i : Fin.t (S k)) (a : A),
    Vector.nth (insert_at k v i a) i = a.
  Proof.
    induction k as [| k ih]; intros v i a.
    + destruct (fin_inv_S _ i) as [hi | (j & hi)]; subst;
      [reflexivity | inversion j].
    + destruct (fin_inv_S _ i) as [hi | (j & hi)]; subst; cbn;
      [reflexivity | apply ih].
  Qed.

Insertion at the first index is cons.
  Lemma insert_at_F1 : ∀ k (w : Vector.t A k) (a : A),
    insert_at k w Fin.F1 a = a :: w.
  Proof.
    intros k w a; destruct k; cbn; [rewrite (vector_inv_0 w); reflexivity | reflexivity].
  Qed.

Overwriting the inserted entry (replace from the standard library) is the same as inserting the new value.
  Lemma replace_insert_at : ∀ k (v : Vector.t A k) (i : Fin.t (S k)) (a b : A),
    replace (insert_at k v i a) i b = insert_at k v i b.
  Proof.
    induction k as [| k ih]; intros v i a b.
    + destruct (fin_inv_S _ i) as [hi | (j & hi)]; subst;
      [reflexivity | inversion j].
    + destruct (fin_inv_S _ i) as [hi | (j & hi)]; subst; cbn;
      [reflexivity | f_equal; apply ih].
  Qed.

Transporting a cons along e : S N = S M transports the tail along the derived equality N = M.
  Lemma rew_cons : ∀ (N M : nat) (e : S N = S M) (a : A) (v : Vector.t A N),
    rew [Vector.t A] e in (a :: v) = a :: rew [Vector.t A] (eq_add_S _ _ e) in v.
  Proof.
    intros N M e a v.
    rewrite (UIP_nat _ _ e (f_equal S (eq_add_S _ _ e))).
    generalize (eq_add_S N M e) as e'; intros e'.
    destruct e'; reflexivity.
  Qed.

Transporting FS i along e : S N = S M transports i along the derived equality N = M.
  Lemma rew_FS : ∀ (N M : nat) (e : S N = S M) (i : Fin.t N),
    rew [Fin.t] e in (Fin.FS i) = Fin.FS (rew [Fin.t] (eq_add_S _ _ e) in i).
  Proof.
    intros N M e i.
    rewrite (UIP_nat _ _ e (f_equal S (eq_add_S _ _ e))).
    generalize (eq_add_S N M e) as e'; intros e'.
    destruct e'; reflexivity.
  Qed.

Appending y :: w to v and transporting along m + S k = S (m + k) is the same as inserting y at index m (that is, Fin.R m F1 transported) into v ++ w.
  Lemma rew_app_cons : ∀ m k (v : Vector.t A m) (w : Vector.t A k) (y : A)
    (e : m + S k = S (m + k)),
    rew [Vector.t A] e in (v ++ y :: w) =
    insert_at (m + k) (v ++ w) (rew [Fin.t] e in Fin.R m (Fin.F1 : Fin.t (S k))) y.
  Proof.
    induction m as [| m ih]; intros k v w y e.
    + rewrite (vector_inv_0 v).
      rewrite (UIP_nat _ _ e eq_refl); cbn.
      rewrite insert_at_F1; reflexivity.
    + destruct (vector_inv_S v) as (b & v' & hv); subst; cbn.
      rewrite rew_cons, rew_FS; cbn.
      f_equal; apply ih.
  Qed.

Transport along a length equality does not change the numeric value of a Fin.t index.
  Lemma rew_to_nat : ∀ (N M : nat) (e : N = M) (i : Fin.t N),
    proj1_sig (Fin.to_nat (rew [Fin.t] e in i)) = proj1_sig (Fin.to_nat i).
  Proof. intros N M e i; destruct e; reflexivity. Qed.

Indices with the same numeric value are equal.
  Lemma fin_eq_of_to_nat : ∀ (N : nat) (i j : Fin.t N),
    proj1_sig (Fin.to_nat i) = proj1_sig (Fin.to_nat j) → i = j.
  Proof.
    intros N i j h; apply Fin.to_nat_inj; exact h.
  Qed.

End Insert.

Interaction with zip_with

Section Zip.
Element types of the two inputs and the output.
  Context {A B C : Type}.

Zipping two vectors in which entries were inserted at the same index is inserting the zipped entry into the zipped vectors.
  Lemma zip_with_insert_at (f : A → B → C) :
    ∀ k (v : Vector.t A k) (w : Vector.t B k) (i : Fin.t (S k)) (a : A) (b : B),
    zip_with f (insert_at k v i a) (insert_at k w i b) =
    insert_at k (zip_with f v w) i (f a b).
  Proof.
    induction k as [| k ih]; intros v w i a b.
    + destruct (fin_inv_S _ i) as [hi | (j & hi)]; subst;
      [rewrite (vector_inv_0 v), (vector_inv_0 w); reflexivity | inversion j].
    + destruct (fin_inv_S _ i) as [hi | (j & hi)]; subst; cbn; [reflexivity |].
      destruct (vector_inv_S v) as (x & v' & hv).
      destruct (vector_inv_S w) as (y & w' & hw).
      subst; cbn; f_equal; apply ih.
  Qed.

zip_with distributes over concatenation of equal-length blocks.
  Lemma zip_with_app (f : A → B → C) :
    ∀ m k (v1 : Vector.t A m) (v2 : Vector.t A k) (w1 : Vector.t B m) (w2 : Vector.t B k),
    zip_with f (v1 ++ v2) (w1 ++ w2) = zip_with f v1 w1 ++ zip_with f v2 w2.
  Proof.
    induction m as [| m ih]; intros k v1 v2 w1 w2.
    + rewrite (vector_inv_0 v1), (vector_inv_0 w1); reflexivity.
    + destruct (vector_inv_S v1) as (x & v1' & hv).
      destruct (vector_inv_S w1) as (y & w1' & hw).
      subst; cbn; f_equal; apply ih.
  Qed.

End Zip.
