Pairwise inequality of discrete logarithms, toy instance

A concrete instance of the inequality protocol of Crypto/NeqSigma.v: a proof of knowledge of x₁, x₂, x₃ with hᵢ = gᵢ^xᵢ such that the xᵢ are pairwise distinct. The file is extracted to OCaml by src/Extraction/NeqSigmalib.
The protocol is the AND-composition of the three Schnorr statements together with, for every pair i < j, an Okamoto proof (Okamoto, CRYPTO 1992) of a representation

gⱼ = (gᵢ·gⱼ)^a · (hᵢ·hⱼ)^b, a = xᵢ/(xᵢ - xⱼ), b = 1/(xⱼ - xᵢ).
Indeed (gᵢ·gⱼ)^a·(hᵢ·hⱼ)^b = gᵢ^(a + b·xᵢ)·gⱼ^(a + b·xⱼ) = gⱼ, and these a, b exist only when xᵢ ≠ xⱼ. All the sub-proofs share one challenge. With three statements there are 3 pairs, hence 3 + 3 = 6 commitments, one challenge and 3 + 3·2 = 9 responses, and the prover needs 9 random scalars.
The group is the order-q subgroup of Z_p^* for the toy safe prime p = 2q + 1 = 5927 with q = 2963 (from Utility/Zpstar.v: Zp q is the scalar field and Schnorr_group p q the group of quadratic residues mod p). The parameters are far too small to be secure; they are chosen so that primality can be proved by trial division (Examples/Prime.v) and the extracted code runs instantly.
The bases are g₁ = 4, g₂ = 7, g₃ = 13 and the secrets x₁ = 3, x₂ = 5, x₃ = 8. Replacing x₂ by x₁ or x₃ (so that two secrets coincide) makes the honest prover's transcript fail verification.

The six commitments: gᵢ^uᵢ for the AND part, then one Okamoto commitment (gᵢ·gⱼ)^v·(hᵢ·hⱼ)^w per pair, from the 9 random scalars us.
  Definition generalised_construct_neq_commitment_ins
    (us : Vector.t (@Zp q) 9) : Vector.t (@Schnorr_group p q) 6.
  Proof.
    refine(@generalised_construct_neq_commitment (@Zp q)
    (@Schnorr_group p q) (@Schnorr.one p q prime_p prime_q)
    (@mul_schnorr_group p q prime_p prime_q)
    (@pow 2 p q safe_prime prime_p prime_q) _
    [g₁; g₂; g₃] [h₁; h₂; h₃] us).
  Defined.

The honest transcript for challenge c: the commitments, c, and the nine responses (three Schnorr responses uᵢ + c·xᵢ, then two Okamoto responses per pair).
  Definition generalised_construct_neq_conversations_real_transcript_ins
    (us : Vector.t (@Zp q) 9) (c : (@Zp q)) :
    @sigma_proto (@Zp q) (@Schnorr_group p q) 6 1 9.
  Proof.
    refine (@generalised_construct_neq_conversations_real_transcript
    (@Zp q) zp_add zp_mul zp_sub zp_inv
    (@Schnorr_group p q) (@Schnorr.one p q prime_p prime_q)
    (@mul_schnorr_group p q prime_p prime_q)
    (@pow 2 p q safe_prime prime_p prime_q) _
    [x₁; x₂; x₃] [g₁; g₂; g₃] [h₁; h₂; h₃] us c).
  Defined.

The non-interactive proof: c is fn applied to p, q, all the pairs (gᵢ, hᵢ) and the commitments; fn is a hash function supplied by the OCaml caller (strong Fiat–Shamir).
  Definition nizk_generalised_construct_neq_conversations_real_transcript_ins
    (fn : ∀ {m : nat}, Vector.t (Z + (@Schnorr_group p q)) m → (@Zp q))
    (us : Vector.t (@Zp q) 9) : @sigma_proto (@Zp q) (@Schnorr_group p q) 6 1 9.
  Proof.
    set (comm := generalised_construct_neq_commitment_ins us).
    set (c := fn _ ([inl p; inl q; inr g₁; inr h₁; inr g₂; inr h₂;
      inr g₃; inr h₃] ++ Vector.map inr comm)).
    exact (generalised_construct_neq_conversations_real_transcript_ins us c).
  Defined.

The verifier: the AND part is accepting for (gᵢ, hᵢ) and every pair's Okamoto transcript is accepting for the bases [gᵢ·gⱼ; hᵢ·hⱼ] and the target gⱼ.
  Definition generalised_neq_accepting_conversations_ins :
    @sigma_proto (@Zp q) (@Schnorr_group p q) 6 1 9 → bool.
  Proof.
    intro pf.
    refine(@generalised_neq_accepting_conversations (@Zp q)
    (@Schnorr_group p q) (@Schnorr.one p q prime_p prime_q)
    (@mul_schnorr_group p q prime_p prime_q)
    (@pow 2 p q safe_prime prime_p prime_q)
    Schnorr.dec_zpstar _ [g₁; g₂; g₃] [h₁; h₂; h₃]
    pf).
  Defined.
