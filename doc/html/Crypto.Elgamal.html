ElGamal Encryption in the Exponent

This file formalises (lifted, or ‘exponential’) ElGamal encryption over an abstract prime-order group and proves its basic correctness and homomorphic properties.
The setting is a vector space G over a field F (in practice a cyclic group of prime order q written multiplicatively, with scalars in Z/qZ); gop is the group operation, ginv the inverse and g ^ a (i.e. gpow g a) scalar multiplication / exponentiation. The key pair is a secret key x : F and a public key h = g ^ x. A message m : F is encrypted in the exponent with randomness r : F:

      enc m r = (g ^ r, g ^ m · h ^ r)
      dec (c₁, c₂) = c₂ · (c₁ ^ x)⁻¹
Decryption therefore recovers the group element g ^ m, not m itself; recovering m requires a discrete-logarithm search over the (small) message space, which is done elsewhere (for example in the tally back-ends). The scheme is additively homomorphic: the component-wise product of two ciphertexts encrypts the sum of the messages, and multiplying by an encryption of zero (re_enc) re-randomises a ciphertext without changing its plaintext.
Main results:
  • dec_is_left_inv_of_enc: decryption inverts encryption (up to the exponent lifting);
  • dec_re_enc_left_inv: re-encryption preserves the plaintext;
  • additive_homomorphic_property: mul_cipher is homomorphic for addition of plaintexts;
  • ballot_encryption_decryption_raise_message and ballot_re_encryption_decryption_raise_message: the same facts lifted point-wise to vectors of ciphertexts (‘ballots’).
Only functional correctness is proved here; no semantic-security (IND-CPA) statement is made. These definitions are used by the proofs of correct encryption (EncProof), correct decryption (DecProof), the distributed variant (DistElgamal), the shuffle proofs and the e-voting tallies.

Encryption, decryption and ciphertext operations

  Section EncDecDef.

enc m r is the ElGamal encryption of g ^ m under public key h with randomness r: the pair (g ^ r, g ^ m · h ^ r).
    Definition enc (m : F) (r : F) : G × G :=
      (g^r, gop (g^m) (h^r)).

dec (c₁, c₂) computes c₂ · (c₁ ^ x)⁻¹ using the secret key x. On an honest ciphertext it yields g ^ m, the message lifted into the group.
    Definition dec (c : G × G) :=
      match c with
      |(c₁, c₂) ⇒ gop c₂ (ginv (c₁^x))
      end.

re_enc (c₁, c₂) r re-randomises a ciphertext by multiplying it component-wise with (g ^ r, h ^ r), an encryption of the neutral message 0. This is the operation applied by a mix server in a re-encryption shuffle.
    Definition re_enc (c : G × G) (r : F) : G × G :=
      match c with
      |(c₁, c₂) ⇒ (gop c₁ (g^r), gop c₂ (h^r))
      end.

mul_cipher is the component-wise product of two ciphertexts, the homomorphic operation corresponding to addition of plaintexts.
    Definition mul_cipher (c₁ c₂ : G × G) : G × G :=
      match c₁, c₂ with
      |(c₁, d₁), (c₂, d₂) ⇒ (gop c₁ c₂, gop d₁ d₂)
      end.

  End EncDecDef.

Ballots: vectors of ciphertexts

A ballot is a length-n vector of ciphertexts (one per candidate or option). The operations below lift enc, dec, re_enc and mul_cipher point-wise to such vectors.
  Section BallotDef.

encrypted_ballot ms rs encrypts the i-th message with the i-th randomness.
      Definition encrypted_ballot {n : nat}
        (ms rs : Vector.t F n) : Vector.t (G × G) n :=
        zip_with (fun m r ⇒ enc m r) ms rs.

decrypted_ballot cs decrypts every component, yielding a vector of group elements g ^ mᵢ.
      Definition decrypted_ballot {n : nat}
        (cs : Vector.t (G × G) n) : Vector.t G n :=
        map (fun c ⇒ dec c) cs.

re_encryption_ballot cs rs re-randomises every component of a ballot with fresh randomness.
      Definition re_encryption_ballot {n : nat}
        (cs : Vector.t (G × G) n) (rs : Vector.t F n) :
        Vector.t (G × G) n :=
        zip_with (fun c r ⇒ re_enc c r) cs rs.

mul_encrypted_ballots us vs is the component-wise homomorphic product of two ballots; used to accumulate ballots into an encrypted tally.
      Definition mul_encrypted_ballots {n : nat}
        (us vs : Vector.t (G × G) n) : Vector.t (G × G) n :=
        zip_with (fun c₁ c₂ ⇒ mul_cipher c₁ c₂) us vs.

raise_message_gen ms lifts each message into the group, map (fun m ⇒ g ^ m) ms; it is the expected result of decrypting encrypted_ballot ms rs.
      Definition raise_message_gen {n : nat}
        (ms : Vector.t F n) : Vector.t G n :=
        map (fun m ⇒ g^m) ms.

  End BallotDef.

Correctness proofs

Single ciphertexts

    Section EncDecProof.

Correctness of decryption: if (c, d) = enc m r then dec (c, d) = g ^ m. Proof: d · (c ^ x)⁻¹ = g ^ m · g ^ (x r) · (g ^ (r x))⁻¹, and commutativity of F cancels the last two factors.
      Lemma dec_is_left_inv_of_enc :
        ∀ (c d : G) (r m : F),
        (c, d) = enc m r → g^m = dec (c, d).
      Proof.
        unfold enc, dec;
        intros ? ? ? ? H;
        inversion H; clear H.
        rewrite Hk.
        rewrite <- !(@vector_space_smul_associative_fmul F (@eq F)
          zero one add mul sub div
          opp inv G (@eq G) gid ginv gop gpow Hvec).
        setoid_rewrite <- (@monoid_is_associative G (@eq G) gop gid).
        rewrite <- commutative.
        rewrite (@commutative_ring_is_commutative F (@eq F)
          zero one opp add sub mul).
        rewrite (@group_is_right_inverse G (@eq G) gop gid ginv).
        rewrite left_identity.
        exact eq_refl.
        typeclasses eauto.
        typeclasses eauto.
        typeclasses eauto.
      Qed.

Re-encryption preserves the plaintext: if (c, d) = enc m r₁ and (e, f) = re_enc (c, d) r₂ then dec (e, f) = g ^ m. The proof collects the randomness into g ^ (x (r₁ + r₂)) and cancels it.
      Lemma dec_re_enc_left_inv :
        ∀ c d e f r₁ r₂ m,
        (c, d) = enc m r₁ →
        (e, f) = re_enc (c, d) r₂ →
        g^m = dec (e, f).
      Proof.
        unfold re_enc, enc, dec.
        intros ? ? ? ? ? ? ? H₁ H₂.
        inversion H₁; clear H₁.
        inversion H₂; clear H₂.
        rewrite Hk in × |- ×.
        subst.
        remember (gop (g ^ r₁) (g ^ r₂) ^ x) as t.
        rewrite <- smul_distributive_fadd in Heqt.
        rewrite <- (@vector_space_smul_associative_fmul
          F (@eq F) zero one add mul sub div
          opp inv G (@eq G) gid ginv gop gpow) in Heqt.
        rewrite (@commutative_ring_is_commutative
          F (@eq F) zero one opp add sub mul) in Heqt.
        rewrite (@ring_is_left_distributive
          F (@eq F) zero one opp add sub mul) in Heqt.
        repeat rewrite smul_pow_up.
        assert (Ht : (gop (gop (g ^ m) (g ^ (x × r₁))) (g ^ (x × r₂)) =
              (gop (g ^ m) (gop (g ^ (x × r₁)) (g ^ (x × r₂)))))).

Additive homomorphism: if (c, d) = enc m₁ r₁ and (e, f) = enc m₂ r₂, then decrypting mul_cipher (c, d) (e, f) yields g ^ (m₁ + m₂). The proof works entirely in the exponent: the product is g ^ (m₁ + m₂ + x r₁ + x r₂) times the inverse of g ^ (x (r₁ + r₂)), and the field identities cancel the randomness terms.
      Lemma additive_homomorphic_property :
        ∀ c d e f r₁ r₂ m₁ m₂,
        (c, d) = enc m₁ r₁ →
        (e, f) = enc m₂ r₂ →
        g^(m₁ + m₂) = dec (mul_cipher (c, d) (e, f)).
      Proof.
        unfold re_enc, enc, dec, mul_cipher.
        intros ? ? ? ? ? ? ? ? H1 H2.
        inversion H1; clear H1;
        inversion H2; clear H2.
        rewrite Hk in × |- ×.
        subst.
        remember (gop (g ^ r₁) (g ^ r₂) ^ x) as t.
        rewrite <- smul_distributive_fadd in Heqt.
        rewrite <- (@vector_space_smul_associative_fmul
          F (@eq F) zero one add mul sub div
          opp inv G (@eq G) gid ginv gop gpow) in Heqt.
        rewrite (@commutative_ring_is_commutative
          F (@eq F) zero one opp add sub mul) in Heqt.
        rewrite (@ring_is_left_distributive
          F (@eq F) zero one opp add sub mul) in Heqt.
        rewrite Heqt.
        rewrite connection_between_vopp_and_fopp.
        repeat rewrite smul_pow_up.
        repeat rewrite <- smul_distributive_fadd.
        assert (Htt: x × r₁ + m₂ = m₂ + x × r₁).
          rewrite commutative; reflexivity.
        rewrite associative.
        assert (Htv: m₁ + x × r₁ + m₂ = m₁ + (x × r₁ + m₂)).
          rewrite associative; reflexivity.
        rewrite Htv, Htt, associative.
        clear Htt; clear Htv; clear Heqt.
        assert(Htt: m₁ + m₂ + x × r₁ + x × r₂ + opp (x × r₁ + x × r₂) =
          m₁ + m₂ + (x × r₁ + x × r₂ + opp (x × r₁ + x × r₂))).
          repeat rewrite associative; reflexivity.
        rewrite Htt, field_zero_iff_right, right_identity;
        reflexivity.
        typeclasses eauto.

        typeclasses eauto.
      Qed.

Ballots

    Section BallotProof.
Ballot-level correctness: if cs = encrypted_ballot ms rs then decrypted_ballot cs = raise_message_gen ms. By induction on the length n, using dec_is_left_inv_of_enc-style reasoning on the head component.
      Lemma ballot_encryption_decryption_raise_message:
        ∀ (n : nat) (ms rs : Vector.t F n) (cs : Vector.t (G × G) n),
        cs = encrypted_ballot ms rs →
        raise_message_gen ms = decrypted_ballot cs.
      Proof.
        induction n.
        + intros ? ? cs →.
          refine (match ms, rs with
            | nil _, nil _ ⇒ _
            end).
          reflexivity.
        + intros ? ? cs →.
          pose proof (vector_inv_S ms) as (hms & tms & Hms).
          pose proof (vector_inv_S rs) as (hrs & trs & Hrs).
          rewrite Hms, Hrs; simpl.
          f_equal.
          rewrite Hk.
          repeat rewrite smul_pow_up,
          smul_pow_mul, <-associative, right_inverse,
          right_identity; reflexivity.
          apply IHn with (rs := tl rs).
          unfold encrypted_ballot.
          rewrite Hrs; simpl.
          reflexivity.
      Qed.
